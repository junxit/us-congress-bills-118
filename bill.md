# H.R. 9045

> To amend title 31, United States Code, to exempt entities subject to taxation under section 528 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 from certain beneficial ownership reporting requirements.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Community Association Reporting Exemption Act”.

## § 2. Exemption

Section 5336(a)(11)(B)(xix) of title 31, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in subclause (II), by striking “or” at the end;
- **(2)** in subclause (III), by adding “or” at the end; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(IV)** entity subject to taxation under section 528 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986;
