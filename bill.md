# H.R. 2115

> To provide for a limitation on availability of funds for Independent Agencies, General Services Administration, Real Property Activities, Federal Building Fund for fiscal year 2024.

## § 1. Limitation on availability of funds for Independent Agencies, General Services Administration, Real Property Activities, Federal Building Fund for fiscal year 2024

Notwithstanding any other provision of the law, amounts authorized to be appropriated or otherwise available for Independent Agencies, General Services Administration, Real Property Activities, Federal Building Fund for fiscal year 2024 may not exceed $9,285,082,000.
