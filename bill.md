# S. 2519

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to impose an asset test on professional sports leagues qualifying for 501(c)(6) status.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Sports League Tax-Exempt Status Limitation Act”.

## § 2. Asset test for professional sports leagues

- **(a)** *In general* Paragraph (6) of section 501(c) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended—
  - **(1)** by inserting
    - **(“(A)”)** after
      - **(“(6)”)** , and
  - **(2)** by adding at the end the following new subparagraph:
    > - **(B)** Subparagraph (A) shall not apply to any professional football league if the average annual value of the assets of such league, as reported on the league's annual return, for any 3-taxable-year period beginning after December 31, 2020, exceeds $500,000,000.

- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to taxable years beginning after December 31, 2023.
