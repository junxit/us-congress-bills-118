# H.R. 8105

> To extend the reporting requirements under the Elie Wiesel Genocide and Atrocities Prevention Act of 2018.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Elie Wiesel Genocide And Atrocities Prevention Extension Act”.

## § 2. Extension of reporting requirements under the Elie Wiesel Genocide and Atrocities Prevention Act of 2018

Section 5(a) of the Elie Wiesel Genocide and Atrocities Prevention Act of 2018 (Public Law 115–441; 132 Stat. 5587) is amended, in the matter preceding paragraph (1), by striking “six years” and inserting “12 years”.
