# H.R. 7251

> To amend the Public Health Service Act to reauthorize certain poison control programs.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Poison Control Centers Reauthorization Act of 2024”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of poison control programs

- **(a)** *National toll-free number and other communication capabilities* Section 1271(c) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 300d–71(c)) is amended by striking “fiscal years 2020 through 2024” and inserting “fiscal years 2025 through 2029”.
- **(b)** *Promoting poison control center utilization* Section 1272(c) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 300d–72(c)) is amended by striking “fiscal years 2020 through 2024” and inserting “fiscal years 2025 through 2029”.
- **(c)** *Poison control center grant program* Section 1273(g) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 300d–73(g)) is amended by striking “fiscal years 2020 through 2024” and inserting “fiscal years 2025 through 2029”.
