# H.R. 4092

> To amend the Public Health Service Act to reauthorize support for residential treatment programs for pregnant and postpartum women.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Protecting Moms and Infants Reauthorization Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Residential treatment programs for pregnant and postpartum women

Section 508(s) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 290bb–1(s)) is amended by striking “$29,931,000 for each of fiscal years 2019 through 2023” and inserting “$38,931,000 for each of fiscal years 2024 through 2028”.
