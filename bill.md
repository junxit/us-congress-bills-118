# H.J. RES. 141

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Department of Labor relating to “Amendment to Prohibited Transaction Exemption 84–24”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Department of Labor relating to “Amendment to Prohibited Transaction Exemption 84–24” (89 Fed. Reg. 32302 (April 25, 2024)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
