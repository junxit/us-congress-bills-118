# S. 3238

> To make a supplemental appropriation to the Secretary of State to carry out the mission and activities of the United States Security Coordinator for Israel and the Palestinian Authority.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “USSC Emergency Supplemental Act”.

## § 2. Supplemental appropriation for United States Security Coordinator for Israel and the Palestinian Authority

In addition to amounts otherwise available, there is appropriated to the Secretary of State, out of amounts in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, $10,000,000, to remain available until expended, to carry out the mission and activities of the United States Security Coordinator for Israel and the Palestinian Authority.
