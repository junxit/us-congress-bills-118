# S. 5518

> To extend the deadline for submitting certain beneficial ownership information reports for companies affected by Hurricane Helene or Milton.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Flexible Compliance for Emergencies and Natural Disasters Act”.

## § 2. Definitions

In this Act:

- **(1)** *Company affected by Hurricane Helene or Milton* The term company affected by Hurricane Helene or Milton means a reporting company—
  - **(A)** formed or registered before, on, or after the date of enactment of this Act; and
  - **(B)** that the Secretary of the Treasury determines is located in an area—
    - **(i)** affected by Hurricane Helene or Milton; and
    - **(ii)** for which the President has declared a major disaster under section 401 of the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act (42 U.S.C. 5170).
- **(2)** *Reporting company* The term reporting company has the meaning given the term in section 5336(a) of title 31, United States Code.

## § 3. Extending the deadline for submitting certain beneficial ownership reports for companies affected by Hurricane Helene or Milton

A company affected by Hurricane Helene or Milton shall not be required to submit any report described in section 5336 of title 31, United States Code, before January 1, 2026.
