# S. 2339

> To extend the prohibition on certain reductions to the B–1 bomber aircraft squadrons.

## § 1. Extension of prohibition on certain reductions to B–1 bomber aircraft squadrons

Section 133(c)(1) of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2022 (Public Law 117–81; 135 Stat. 1574) is amended by striking “September 30, 2023” and inserting “September 30, 2026”.
