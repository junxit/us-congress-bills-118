# S. 4142

> To increase the penalty for prohibited possession of a phone in a correctional facility.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Lieutenant Osvaldo Albarati Stopping Prison Contraband Act”.

## § 2. Prohibited possession of a phone

Section 1791(b) of title 18, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (3), by inserting “or (d)(1)(F)” after “(d)(1)(B)”; and
- **(2)** in paragraph (4), by striking “, (d)(1)(E), or (d)(1)(F)” and inserting “or (d)(1)(E)”.
