# H.R. 10285

> To amend chapter 63 of title 5, United States Code, to modify the accumulation of annual leave for use in succeeding years for administrative law judges.

## § 1. Limits on accumulation of annual leave for administrative law judges

- **(a)** *In general* Section 6304(f)(1) of title 5, United States Code, is amended—
  - **(1)** in subparagraph (G), by striking “; or” and inserting a semicolon;
  - **(2)** in the first subparagraph (H) (relating to a position in the Library of Congress), by striking the period at the end;
  - **(3)** by redesignating the second subparagraph (H) (relating to a position in the United States Secret Service Uniformed Division) as subparagraph (I);
  - **(4)** in subparagraph (I), as so redesignated, by striking the period and inserting “; or”; and
  - **(5)** by adding at the end the following new subparagraph:
    > - **(J)** an administrative law judge position under section 3105.

- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall take effect on the first day of the first applicable pay period beginning on or after the date that is 30 days after the date of enactment of this Act.
