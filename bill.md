# H.R. 8159

> To prohibit the breaching of federally operated dams if such breach would result in the replacement energy resource occupying additional acreage, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Abandoning Inconsistent and Rash Environmental Solutions Act” or the “ACRES Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on breaching of certain dams

- **(a)** *In general* The Secretary of the Army may not breach any federally operated dam if the energy resource intended to replace such dam as a result of such breach would occupy an area of land that is larger in acreage than the area occupied by such dam by at least 5 percent.
- **(b)** *Study required* If the Secretary considers breaching a federally operated dam, the Secretary, in coordination with the Secretary of the Interior, shall conduct a study analyzing the number of acres of land such dam occupies.
