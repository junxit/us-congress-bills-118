# S. 3542

> To amend the Atchafalaya National Heritage Area Act to modify the boundary of the Atchafalaya National Heritage Area, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Atchafalaya National Heritage Area Boundary Modification Act”.

## § 2. Atchafalaya National Heritage Area Boundary Modification

- **(a)** *Boundaries* Section 213(b) of the Atchafalaya National Heritage Area Act (54 U.S.C. 120102 note; Public Law 109–338; 120 Stat. 1791) is amended by striking “and Ascension” and inserting “Ascension, and Lafourche”.
- **(b)** *Composition of local coordinating entity* Section 213(c)(2) of the Atchafalaya National Heritage Area Act (54 U.S.C. 120102 note; Public Law 109–338; 120 Stat. 1791) is amended by striking “14” and inserting “15”.
