# S. 3614

> To designate the outpatient clinic of the Department of Veterans Affairs in Vallejo, California, as the “Delphine Metcalf-Foster VA Clinic”.

## § 1. Designation of Department of Veterans Affairs outpatient clinic in Vallejo, California

- **(a)** *Designation* The outpatient clinic of the Department of Veterans Affairs in Vallejo, California, shall after the date of the enactment of this Act be known and designated as the “Delphine Metcalf-Foster VA Clinic”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in any law, regulation, map, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the clinic referred to in subsection (a) shall be considered to be a reference to the Delphine Metcalf-Foster VA Clinic.
