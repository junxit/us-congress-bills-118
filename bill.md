# S.J. RES. 99

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Council on Environmental Quality relating to “National Environmental Policy Act Implementing Regulations Revisions Phase 2”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Council on Environmental Quality relating to “National Environmental Policy Act Implementing Regulations Revisions Phase 2” (89 Fed. Reg. 35442 (May 1, 2024)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
