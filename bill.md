# S. 2947

> To expand the transactions for which declarations may be required by the Committee on Foreign Investment in the United States to include investments in United States businesses that maintain or collect sensitive personal data.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Protecting Sensitive Personal Data Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Expansion of declarations required by the Committee on Foreign Investment in the United States

Section 721(b)(1)(C)(v)(IV)(cc) of the Defense Production Act of 1950 (50 U.S.C. 4565(b)(1)(C)(v)(IV)(cc)) is amended by striking “subsection (a)(4)(B)(iii)(II)” and inserting “subclause (II) or (III) of subsection (a)(4)(B)(iii)”.
