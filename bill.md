# S. 2717

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 231 North Franklin Street in Greensburg, Indiana, as the “Brigadier General John T. Wilder Post Office”.

## § 1. Brigadier General John T. Wilder Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 231 North Franklin Street in Greensburg, Indiana, shall be known and designated as the “Brigadier General John T. Wilder Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Brigadier General John T. Wilder Post Office”.
