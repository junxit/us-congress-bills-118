# H.R. 4292

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 774 Sea Island Pkwy, in Saint Helena Island, South Carolina, as the “Laura Matilda Towne Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Laura Matilda Towne Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 774 Sea Island Pkwy, in Saint Helena Island, South Carolina, shall be known and designated as the “Laura Matilda Towne Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Laura Matilda Towne Post Office Building”.
