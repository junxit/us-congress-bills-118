# H.R. 5593

> To amend the Trade Act of 1974 to exempt from the Generalized System of Preferences certain tire articles, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Exclusion of import-sensitive tire articles

Section 503(b)(1) of the Trade Act of 1974 (19 U.S.C. 2463(b)(1)) is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating subparagraph (G) as subparagraph (H); and
- **(2)** by inserting after subparagraph (F) the following new subparagraph:
  > - **(G)** Import-sensitive tire articles.
