# S. 351

> To designate 6 creeks in the State of North Carolina in honor of the lives lost in a plane crash in Carteret County, North Carolina, on February 13, 2022, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Down East Remembrance Act”.

## § 2. Designation of certain creeks, North Carolina

- **(a)** *Designation of Noah Styron Creek*
  - **(1)** *In general* The creek located at latitude 34°59′49.33″ N, longitude 76°8′42.11″ W, shall be known and designated as “Noah Styron Creek”.
  - **(2)** *References* Any reference in a law, regulation, map, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the creek described in paragraph (1) shall be deemed to be a reference to “Noah Styron Creek”.
- **(b)** *Designation of Hunter Parks Creek*
