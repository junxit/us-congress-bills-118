# H.R. 2042

> To provide for a limitation on availability of funds for Related Agencies, National Council on Disability for fiscal year 2024.

## § 1. Limitation on availability of funds for Related Agencies, National Council on Disability for fiscal year 2024

Notwithstanding any other provision of the law, amounts authorized to be appropriated or otherwise available for Related Agencies, National Council on Disability for fiscal year 2024 may not exceed $3,250,000.
