# H.R. 5987

> To amend the Adult Education and Family Literacy Act and the Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act to strengthen adult education.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Adult Education Workforce Opportunity and Reskilling for Knowledge and Success Act” or the “Adult Education WORKS Act”.
