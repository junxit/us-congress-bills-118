# H.R. 5507

> To prohibit the use of Federal funds to establish a Disinformation Governance Board.

## § 1. Prohibition on establishment of a Disinformation Governance Board

No Federal funds authorized to be appropriated or otherwise made available to the Secretary of Homeland Security may be made available for the establishment of a Disinformation Governance Board.
