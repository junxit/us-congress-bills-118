# S. RES. 332

> Prohibiting the imposition of vaccination, testing, and masking requirements relating to COVID–19 for Senate Pages.

## § 1. Prohibition on COVID–19 vaccination, testing, and masking requirements for Senate Pages

A Senate Page or applicant to be a Senate Page may not be required to—

- **(1)** show proof of COVID–19 vaccination status;
- **(2)** receive a vaccination for COVID–19;
- **(3)** undergo testing for COVID–19 without cause; or
- **(4)** wear a mask.
