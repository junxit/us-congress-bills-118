# H.R. 9087

> To require the Inspector General of the Department of Health and Human Services to investigate Head Start facilities that fail to produce their annual audits as required by law.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Head Start Accountability Act of 2024”.

## § 2. Required investigation of certain Head Start facilities

Beginning in 2024, the Inspector General of the Department of Health and Human Services shall investigate Head Start facilities that fail to produce their annual audits as required by law.
