# S. 4197

> To amend the FISA Amendments Act of 2008 to provide for an extension of certain authorities under title VII of the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act of 1978.

## § 1. Extension of certain authorities

- **(a)** *Extension*
  - **(1)** *In general* Section 403(b) of the FISA Amendments Act of 2008 (Public Law 110–261; 122 Stat. 2474) is amended, by striking “April 19, 2024” each place the term appears and inserting “May 17, 2024”.
  - **(2)** *Effective date* The amendments made by paragraph (1) shall take effect as if enacted on April 18, 2024.
- **(b)** *Technical and conforming amendment* Section 404(b)(1) of the FISA Amendments Act of 2008 (Public Law 110–261; 122 Stat. 2476), is amended, in the heading, by striking “April 19, 2024” and inserting “May 17, 2024”.
