# S. 1616

> To amend title 28, United States Code, to allow for 12 associate justices of the Supreme Court of the United States.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Judiciary Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Number of justices; quorum

Section 1 of title 28, United States Code, is amended by striking “a Chief Justice of the United States and eight associate justices, any six of whom shall constitute a quorum” and inserting “a Chief Justice of the United States and 12 associate justices, any 8 of whom shall constitute a quorum”.
