# H.J. RES. 203

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the National Labor Relations Board relating to “Representation-Case Procedures: Election Bars; Proof of Majority Support in Construction Industry Collective-Bargaining Relationships”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the National Labor Relations Board relating to “Representation-Case Procedures: Election Bars; Proof of Majority Support in Construction Industry Collective-Bargaining Relationships” (89 Fed. Reg. 62952 (August 1, 2024)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
