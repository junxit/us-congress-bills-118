# H.R. 2263

> To provide for a limitation on availability of funds for US Department of Agriculture, Farm Production and Conservation Business Center, Salaries and Expenses for fiscal year 2024.

## § 1. Limitation on availability of funds for US Department of Agriculture, Farm Production and Conservation Business Center, Salaries and Expenses for fiscal year 2024

Notwithstanding any other provision of the law, amounts authorized to be appropriated or otherwise available for US Department of Agriculture, Farm Production and Conservation Business Center, Salaries and Expenses for fiscal year 2024 may not exceed $216,350,000.
