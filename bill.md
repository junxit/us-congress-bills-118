# H.R. 1900

> To provide for a limitation on availability of funds for Joint Items, Office of Congressional Workplace Rights for fiscal year 2024.

## § 1. Limitation on availability of funds for Joint Items, Office of Congressional Workplace Rights for fiscal year 2024

Notwithstanding any other provision of the law, amounts authorized to be appropriated or otherwise available for Joint Items, Office of Congressional Workplace Rights for fiscal year 2024 may not exceed $6,332,670,000.
