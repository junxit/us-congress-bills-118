# H.R. 3097

> To amend the Bridgeport Indian Colony Land Trust, Health, and Economic Development Act of 2012 to remove the restriction on class II gaming on certain land in California.

## § 1. Removal of Class II gaming restriction

Section 2(d) of the Bridgeport Indian Colony Land Trust, Health, and Economic Development Act of 2012 (Public Law 112–212) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “class II gaming or”; and
- **(2)** by striking “those terms are” and inserting “that term is”.
