# H.R. 1863

> To provide for a limitation on availability of funds for Senate, U.S. Senate Caucus on International Narcotics Control Expenses for fiscal year 2024.

## § 1. Limitation on availability of funds for Senate, U.S. Senate Caucus on International Narcotics Control Expenses for fiscal year 2024

Notwithstanding any other provision of the law, amounts authorized to be appropriated or otherwise available for Senate, U.S. Senate Caucus on International Narcotics Control Expenses for fiscal year 2024 may not exceed $508,000.
