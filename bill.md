# H.R. 6734

> To prohibit the use of Federal funds to finalize, implement, or enforce proposed ATF Rule 2022R–17, entitled “Definition of “Engaged in the Business” as a Dealer in Firearms”.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Stopping Unconstitutional Background Checks Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on use of Federal funds to finalize, implement, or enforce proposed ATF Rule 2022R–17, entitled “Definition of “Engaged in the Business” as a Dealer in Firearms”

No Federal funds may be used to finalize, implement, or enforce the rule proposed by the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, and Explosives, entitled “Definition of “Engaged in the Business” as a Dealer in Firearms”, in the notice of proposed rulemaking 2022R–17, signed by the Attorney General on August 30, 2023, or any substantially similar rule.
