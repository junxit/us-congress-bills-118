# S. 3742

> To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to establish a community college and career training grant program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Assisting Community Colleges in Educating Skilled Students to Careers Act of 2024” or the “ACCESS Act of 2024”.
