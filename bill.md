# H.R. 1329

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to provide for an increase in the maximum number of judges who may be appointed to the United States Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims.

## § 1. Increase in maximum number of judges appointed to United States Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims

Section 7253(a) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “seven” and inserting “nine”.
