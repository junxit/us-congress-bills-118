# H.R. 7445

> To prohibit the Administrator of the Federal Aviation Administration from authorizing airport space to house undocumented migrants, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Airport Immigration Enforcement Act”.

## § 2. Housing of undocumented migrants

The Administrator of the Federal Aviation Administration may not authorize airport space to house undocumented migrants.

## § 3. Prohibiting funding for housing of undocumented migrants at airports

No Federal funds may be used to house undocumented migrants in any airport.
