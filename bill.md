# H.R. 5544

> To amend title 31, United States Code, to eliminate exceptions to the provisions of such title commonly referred to as the Miscellaneous Receipts Act, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Upholding the Power of the Purse Act”.

## § 2. Elimination of exceptions to Miscellaneous Receipts Act

- **(a)** *In general* Section 3302 of title 31, United States Code, is amended—
  - **(1)** in subsection (a) by striking “Except as provided by another law” and inserting “Except as provided in subsection (c)(3)”; and
  - **(2)** in subsection (c), by add at the end the following:
    > - **(3)** The requirement in subsection (b) shall not apply to any Federal program under which the Government is a custodian of money for States or units of local government, or under which the Government disperses amounts to States or units of local government.

- **(b)** *Application* The amendments made by subsection (a) shall begin to apply in fiscal year 2024.
