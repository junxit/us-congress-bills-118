# H.R. 5948

> To terminate the Office of Gun Violence Prevention in the Executive Office of the President and prohibit the establishment of any similar successor office, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Termination of the Office of Gun Violence Prevention

- **(a)** *In general* The Office of Gun Violence Prevention in the Executive Office of the President shall be terminated as of the date of the enactment of this Act.
- **(b)** *Prohibition on successor offices* The President may not establish any successor office to the Office of Gun Violence Prevention.
- **(c)** *Limit use of Federal funds* No Federal funds may be obligated or expended for the salaries or expenses of any officer or employee of an office described in subsection (a) or (b).
