# H.R. 3052

> To amend title 40, United States Code, to add certain counties to the definition of “Appalachian region”.

## § 1. Definition of Appalachian region

Section 14102(a)(1)(K) of title 40, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by inserting “Hickman, Humphreys,” after “Hawkins,”; and
- **(2)** by inserting “Perry,” after “Overton,”.
