# S. 4439

> To require the Assistant Secretary of Financial Markets of the Department of the Treasury to brief certain Senate committees on the debt issuance of the Department.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Transparency in Debt Issuance Act of 2024”.

## § 2. Briefings

- **(a)** *In general* The Assistant Secretary for Financial Markets of the Department of the Treasury shall brief the Committee on Finance and the Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs of the Senate on a quarterly basis about the debt issuance of the Department, including a summary of recommendations made by the Treasury Borrowing Advisory Committee.
- **(b)** *Timing* The briefings required under subsection (a) should coincide with the publication of the quarterly refunding report of the Department of the Treasury.
