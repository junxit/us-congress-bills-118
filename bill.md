# H.R. 7086

> To provide that certain standards relating to traffic safety campaign messages shall have no force or effect.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Highway Administration Humor Allowance Act”.

## § 2. Traffic safety campaign messages

Provision 04 of section 2L.07 of the Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices for Streets and Highways, as incorporated by reference under the final rule of the Federal Highway Administration titled “National Standards for Traffic Control Devices; the Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices for Streets and Highways; Revision” and issued December 19, 2023 (88 Fed. Reg. 87672), shall have no force or effect.
