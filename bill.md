# H.R. 2296

> To provide for a limitation on availability of funds for Related Agency and Food and Drug Administration, Buildings and Facilities for fiscal year 2024.

## § 1. Limitation on availability of funds for Related Agency and Food and Drug Administration, Buildings and Facilities for fiscal year 2024

Notwithstanding any other provision of the law, amounts authorized to be appropriated or otherwise available for Related Agency and Food and Drug Administration, Buildings and Facilities for fiscal year 2024 may not exceed $11,788,000.
