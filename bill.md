# H.R. 4060

> To amend the Fiscal Responsibility Act of 2023 to repeal the sunset of the work requirement exemption applicable to homeless individuals, veterans, and foster care individuals for the purpose of determining eligibility to receive supplemental nutrition assistance program benefits under the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Food Access and Stability Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Amendment

Section 311(b)(2) of title II of division C of the Fiscal Responsibility Act of 2023 (Public Law 118–5) is amended by striking “subsection (a)” and inserting “subsection (a)(1)”.
