# H.R. 6215

> To prohibit Federal funds from being made available for the Justice40 Initiative or for the American Climate Corps.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “No American Climate Corps Act”.

## § 2. American Climate Corps and Justice40 Initiative funding prohibition

No Federal funds may be used for—

- **(1)** the Justice40 Initiative (as described in Executive Order 14008 (86 Fed. Reg. 7619, relating to tackling the climate crisis)); or
- **(2)** the American Climate Corps (as described in the fact sheet published by the White House on September 20, 2023, relating to the American Climate Corps).
