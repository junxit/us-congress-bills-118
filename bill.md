# H.R. 3256

> To provide that Executive Order 14096 shall have no force or effect.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Preventing Executive Climate Overreach Act”.

## § 2. Executive Order 14096

Executive Order 14096 (88 Fed. Reg. 25251; relating to revitalizing our Nation's commitment to environmental justice for all) shall have no force or effect.
