# H.J. RES. 191

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Department of Energy relating to “Loan Guarantees for Clean Energy Projects”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Department of Energy relating to “Loan Guarantees for Clean Energy Projects” (88 Fed. Reg. 34419 (May 30, 2023)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
