# H.R. 6227

> To amend the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act to expand the tropical disease product priority review voucher program to encourage prevention and treatment of coccidioidomycosis.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Adding Coccidioidomycosis to the FDA Priority Review Voucher Program Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Expanding tropical disease product priority review voucher program to encourage prevention and treatment of coccidioidomycosis

Section 524(a)(3) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (21 U.S.C. 360n(a)(3)) is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating subparagraph (S) as subparagraph (T); and
- **(2)** by inserting after subparagraph (R) the following:
  > - **(S)** Coccidioidomycosis.
