# H.J. RES. 215

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the United States Fish and Wildlife Service relating to “Migratory Bird Hunting; 2024–25 Seasons for Certain Migratory Game Birds”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the United States Fish and Wildlife Service relating to “Migratory Bird Hunting; 2024–25 Seasons for Certain Migratory Game Birds” (89 Fed. Reg. 70545; published August 30, 2024), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
