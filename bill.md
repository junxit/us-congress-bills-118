# H.R. 2914

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the health coverage tax credit.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Health Coverage Tax Credit Reauthorization Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Extension of health coverage tax credit

- **(a)** *In general* Section 35(b)(1)(B) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “before January 1, 2022” and inserting “before January 1, 2028”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to months beginning after December 31, 2021.
