# H.R. 3447

> To amend title 23, United States Code, to authorize a hydrogen powered vehicle to exceed certain weight limits on the Interstate Highway System, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Hydrogen fuel cell vehicles

Section 127(s) of title 23, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in the subsection heading by striking “gas and electric battery vehicles” and inserting “gas, electric battery vehicles, and hydrogen fuel cell vehicles”; and
- **(2)** by inserting “or fueled primarily by hydrogen fuel cells, or fueled or powered by a combination thereof,” before “may exceed”.
