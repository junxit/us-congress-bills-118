# H.R. 4042

> To amend title 11, District of Columbia Official Code, to prohibit the exclusion of individuals from service on a District of Columbia jury on account of sexual orientation or gender identity.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “District of Columbia Local Juror Non-Discrimination Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Exclusion from District of Columbia juries on account of sexual orientation or gender identity prohibited

- **(a)** *Prohibiting discrimination* Section 11–1903, District of Columbia Official Code, is amended by inserting “(including sexual orientation or gender identity)” after “sex”.
- **(b)** *No effect on other remedies* Section 11–1910(c), District of Columbia Official Code, is amended by inserting “(including sexual orientation or gender identity)” after “sex”.
