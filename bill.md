# H.R. 7208

> To reauthorize the Traumatic Brain Injury program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Traumatic Brain Injury Program Reauthorization Act of 2024”.

## § 2. Prevention and control of injuries

Section 394A(b) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 280b–3(b)) is amended by striking “2020 through 2024” and inserting “2025 through 2029”.

## § 3. State grants for projects regarding traumatic brain injury

Section 1252(i) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 300d–52(i)) is amended by striking “2020 through 2024” and inserting “2025 through 2029”.

## § 4. State grants for protection and advocacy services

Section 1253(l) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 300d–53(l)) is amended by striking “2020 through 2024” and inserting “2025 through 2029”.
