# S. 3574

> To amend chapter 3 of title 36, United States Code, to designate the mastodon as the national fossil of the United States.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “National Fossil Act”.

## § 2. National fossil of the United States

- **(a)** *Designation* Chapter 3 of title 36, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:
  > ## § 306. National fossil
  >
  > The fossil of the genus Mammut, commonly known as the mastodon, is the national fossil.

- **(b)** *Conforming amendments* The table of sections for chapter 3 of title 36, United States Code, is amended—
