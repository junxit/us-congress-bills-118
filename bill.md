# H. RES. 1561

> Condemning President Biden’s remarks referring to President Trump’s supporters as “garbage”.

## Preamble

- Whereas, on October 29, 2024, President Biden stated, “The only garbage I see floating around there is his supporters”, referring to supporters of President Trump: Now, therefore, be it

That the House of Representatives condemns President Biden’s remarks referring to President Trump’s supporters as “garbage”.
