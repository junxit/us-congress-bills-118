# H.R. 868

> To shorten the review period for the congressional review of termination of certain national emergencies, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Termination review of national emergencies by Congress

Section 202(b) of the National Emergencies Act (50 U.S.C. 1622(b)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “to consider a vote” and inserting “to vote”; and
- **(2)** by striking “whether that emergency shall be terminated” and inserting “whether such emergency declaration shall continue to be in effect”.
