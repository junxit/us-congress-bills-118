# H.R. 9318

> To amend the Older Americans Act of 1965 to make available to Guam, American Samoa, the Virgin Islands of the United States, and the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands additional funds for community service senior opportunities.

## § 1. Amendments

Section 506(a)(2) of the Older Americans Act of 1965 (42 U.S.C. 3056d(a)(2)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “0.75 percent” and inserting “1.0 percent”,
- **(2)** in subparagraph (A) by striking “30 percent” and inserting “25 percent”, and
- **(3)** in subparagraph (B) by striking “10 percent” and inserting “25 percent”.

## § 2. Application of amendments

The amendments made by this Act shall not apply with respect to funds appropriated before the date of the enactment of this Act.
