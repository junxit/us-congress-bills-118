# H.R. 5609

> To prohibit the use of Federal funds for security protection for the former Director of the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases who served between January 1984 and December 2022.

## § 1. No Federal funds for security protection for the former Director of the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases who served between January 1984 and December 2022

No Federal funds may be used for U.S. Marshal protection, or any other security protection, for the former Director of the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases who served between January 1984 and December 2022.
