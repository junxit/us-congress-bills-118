# H.R. 5347

> To give preferential consideration to grant applicants that are using such funds for school resource officers, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Protecting Our Kids Act”.

## § 2. Preferential consideration

Section 1701(c) of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 (34 U.S.C. 10381(c)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (2)(C), by striking “or” at the end;
- **(2)** in paragraph (3)(B), by striking the period and inserting “; or”; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(4)** for the purposes described in subsection (b)(12).
