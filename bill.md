# S. 1872

> To repeal the 2001 Authorization for Use of Military Force.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “End Endless Wars Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of 2001 Authorization for Use of Military Force

The Authorization for Use of Military Force (Public Law 107–40; 115 Stat. 224; 50 U.S.C. 1541 note) is repealed effective 180 days after the date of the enactment of this Act.
