# H.R. 9552

> To amend title 49, United States Code, to exempt ticket agents that are small businesses from the requirement to provide refunds for cancelled or significantly delayed or changed flights, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Flight Refund Fairness Act”.

## § 2. Refunds for cancelled or significantly delayed or changed flights

Section 42305(e) of title 49, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(4)** *Special rule for small businesses* This subsection shall not apply to a ticket agent that is a small business (as determined by the Administrator of the Small Business Administration).
