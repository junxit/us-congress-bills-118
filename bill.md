# H.R. 9483

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to prohibit Department of Defense special Government employees from obligating or expending Federal funds while carrying out their official duties, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Preventing Corruption in Advisory Roles Act”.

## § 2. Prohibiting DOD special Government employees from spending Federal funds

- **(a)** *In general* Chapter 81 of title 10, United States Code, is amended by inserting after section 1589 the following:
  > ## § 1590. Prohibition on special Government employees from obligations or expending Federal funds
  >
  > Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a special Government employee (as that term is defined in section 202(a) of title 18) occupying a position in the Department of Defense may not, while carrying out the official duties of the employee’s position, obligate or expend any Federal funds.
