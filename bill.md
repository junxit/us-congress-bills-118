# H.R. 6922

> To amend section 242 of the National Housing Act to provide parity with respect to access to the mortgage insurance for hospitals program for licensed hospitals, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Securing Facilities for Mental Health Services Act”.

## § 2. Mortgage insurance for hospitals parity

- **(a)** *In general* Section 242(b)(1) of the National Housing Act (12 U.S.C. 1715z–7(b)(1)) is amended—
  - **(1)** in subparagraph (A), by striking the semicolon at the end and inserting “; and”;
  - **(2)** by striking subparagraph (B); and
  - **(3)** by redesignating subparagraph (C) as subparagraph (B).
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply upon the date of the enactment of this Act.
