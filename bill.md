# H.R. 7500

> To amend the Defense Production Act of 1950 to allow an Indian Tribe to request CFIUS review of certain transactions between the Indian Tribe and foreign persons, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Requests by Indian Tribes for CFIUS review

Section 721(a)(4) of the Defense Production Act of 1950 (50 U.S.C. 4565(a)(4)) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(G)** *Requests by Indian Tribes for review of transactions* Upon the written request of an Indian Tribe (as defined in section 4(e) of the Indian Self-Determination and Education Assistance Act, (25 U.S.C. 5304(e))) with respect to a transaction between such Indian Tribe and a foreign person (other than an Indian Tribe), this section shall apply—
>   - **(i)** to such transaction in the same manner as this section applies to a covered transaction; and
>   - **(ii)** to the business or real estate that is the subject of such transaction in the same manner as this section applies to a United States business or real estate located in the United States.
