# S.J. RES. 79

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Department of Labor relating to “Retirement Security Rule: Definition of an Investment Advice Fiduciary”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Department of Labor relating to “Retirement Security Rule: Definition of an Investment Advice Fiduciary” (89 Fed. Reg. 32122 (April 25, 2024)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
