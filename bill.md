# H.R. 10434

> To amend the Consumer Product Safety Act to increase reliance by the Consumer Product Safety Commission on voluntary consumer product safety standards, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Consumer Product Sanity Act”.

## § 2. Increasing reliance by Commission on voluntary consumer product safety standards

Section 7(b) of the Consumer Product Safety Act (15 U.S.C. 2056(b)) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(3)** In any case in which the Commission determines that compliance with a voluntary consumer product safety standard would eliminate or adequately reduce the risk of injury addressed, but determines substantial compliance with such voluntary standard is not likely, the Commission may promulgate a consumer product safety standard addressing such risk of injury only if—
>   - **(A)** such consumer product safety standard is identical to such voluntary standard; or
>   - **(B)** in the case of a consumer product safety standard that allows for compliance in more than one manner, at least one such manner of compliance is identical to such voluntary standard.
