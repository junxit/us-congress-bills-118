# S. 2527

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to authorize the Secretary of Defense or the Secretary of a military department to enter into cooperative agreements to manage certain recreational resources in California.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Military Recreational Land Act of 2023 ”.
