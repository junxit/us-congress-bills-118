# H.J. RES. 120

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Financial Stability Oversight Council related to “Guidance on Nonbank Financial Company Determinations”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Financial Stability Oversight Council related to “Guidance on Nonbank Financial Company Determinations” (88 Fed. Reg. 80110; published November 17, 2023), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
