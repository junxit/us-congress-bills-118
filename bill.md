# H.R. 4916

> To amend the Inflation Reduction Act of 2022 to repeal restrictions on onshore wind and solar.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Comprehensive Legislation for Expanding and Advancing Nonrestrictive Energy Act” or the “CLEAN Energy Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of onshore wind and solar leasing restrictions

Paragraph (1) of section 50265(b) of Public Law 117–169 (43 U.S.C. 3006(b)) is repealed.
