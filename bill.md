# H.R. 2079

> To provide for a limitation on availability of funds for The Judiciary, Supreme Court, Care of the Building and Grounds for fiscal year 2024.

## § 1. Limitation on availability of funds for The Judiciary, Supreme Court, Care of the Building and Grounds for fiscal year 2024

Notwithstanding any other provision of the law, amounts authorized to be appropriated or otherwise available for The Judiciary, Supreme Court, Care of the Building and Grounds for fiscal year 2024 may not exceed $14,434,000.
