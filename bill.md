# S. 3061

> To revoke the waiver determination submitted to Congress on September 11, 2023, with respect to certain sanctions imposed with respect to Iran.

## § 1. Revocation of waiver determination with respect to certain Iran sanctions

- **(a)** *In general* On and after the date of the enactment of this Act, the waiver determination issued by the Secretary of State pursuant the provisions of law specified in subsection (b) and submitted to Congress on September 11, 2023, shall have no force or effect.
- **(b)** *Provisions of law specified* The provisions of law specified in this subsection are—
  - **(1)** section 1245(d)(5) of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2012 (22 U.S.C. 8513a(d)(5)); or
  - **(2)** section 1244(i) or 1247(f) of the Iran Freedom and Counter-Proliferation Act of 2012 (22 U.S.C. 8803(i) and 8806(f)).
