# S. 2933

> To reauthorize the program of first responder training.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Safe Response Act”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of program

Section 546(h) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 290ee–1(h)) is amended by striking “$36,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2019 through 2023” and by inserting “$57,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2024 through 2028”.
