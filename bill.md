# H.R. 9116

> To make ineligible for asylum any alien who commits certain actions against the National Guard, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Aliens who commit certain actions against National Guard

Section 208 of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1158) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(f)** *Prohibited on asylum* Any alien who causes the destruction of any defensive barrier on the United States border or uses force against a member of the National Guard—
>   - **(1)** shall be ineligible to apply for asylum or any other status; and
>   - **(2)** shall be subject to expedited removal not later than 72 hours after the alien is apprehended.
