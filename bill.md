# H.R. 8139

> To amend the FAST Act to permit local governments to enter into cooperative procurement contracts for the purchase of rolling stock and related equipment, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Cooperation in Local Procurement Act”.

## § 2. Innovative procurement

Section 3019 of the FAST Act (49 U.S.C. 5325 note) is amended—

- **(1)** by inserting “or local” before “government” each place it appears; and
- **(2)** in subsection (b)(2) in the paragraph heading by inserting “or local government” before “cooperative procurement”.
