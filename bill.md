# H.J. RES. 145

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Food and Drug Administration relating to “Medical Devices; Laboratory Developed Tests”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Food and Drug Administration relating to “Medical Devices; Laboratory Developed Tests” (89 Fed. Reg. 37286; May 6, 2024), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
