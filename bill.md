# H.R. 2333

> To provide for a limitation on availability of funds for U.S. Agency for International Development, Office of the Inspector General for fiscal year 2024.

## § 1. Limitation on availability of funds for U.S. Agency for International Development, Office of the Inspector General for fiscal year 2024

Notwithstanding any other provision of the law, amounts authorized to be appropriated or otherwise available for U.S. Agency for International Development, Office of the Inspector General for fiscal year 2024 may not exceed $76,600,000.
