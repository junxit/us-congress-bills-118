> To establish programs to address addiction and overdoses caused by illicit fentanyl and other opioids, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title; table of contents

## § 2. Definitions

In this Act:

- **(1)** *Fentanyl-related substance* The term fentanyl-related substance has the meaning given the term in section 1308.11(h)(30)(i) of title 21, Code of Federal Regulations (or successor regulations).
- **(2)** *Secretary* The term Secretary means the Secretary of Health and Human Services.
