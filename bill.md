# H. RES. 924

> Recognizing December 17, 2023, as the 30th anniversary of when the first B–2 stealth bomber touched down at Whiteman Air Force Base in Missouri.

## Preamble

- Whereas, on December 17, 1993, the first B–2 stealth bomber touched down at Whiteman Air Force Base in Missouri; and
- Whereas the B–2 stealth bomber is capable of delivering conventional and nuclear munitions at any time anywhere in the world: Now, therefore, be it

That the House of Representatives—

- **(1)** Recognizes as the 30th anniversary of when the first B–2 stealth bomber touched down at Whiteman Air Force Base in Missouri; and
- **(2)** acknowledges that the B–2 stealth bomber represents a vital part of the nuclear triad of the United States and is critical to the United States to project deterrence across the globe.
