## § 2. Definitions

In this Act:

- **(1)** *Appropriate congressional committees* Except as provided in sections 125(b), 210, 211, 303, 313, 323, 411, and 452, part IV of subtitle B of title IV, and title VI, the term appropriate congressional committees means—
  - **(A)** the Committee on Foreign Relations of the Senate; and
  - **(B)** the Committee on Foreign Affairs of the House of Representatives.
- **(2)** *PRC* The term PRC means the People’s Republic of China.
- **(3)** *Secretary* The term Secretary means the Secretary of State.
