# S. 2338

> To provide an additional $30,000,000 for the testing of hypersonic weapon systems with the B–1 bomber, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Additional funding for testing of hypersonic weapon systems with B–1 bomber

- **(a)** *Authorization of appropriations* There is authorized to be appropriated to the Department of Defense for the testing of hypersonic weapon systems with the B–1 bomber $30,000,000 for fiscal year 2024.
- **(b)** *Supplement, not supplant* The amount authorized to be appropriated by subsection (a) shall supplement and not supplant amounts otherwise authorized to be appropriated for the purpose described in that subsection.
