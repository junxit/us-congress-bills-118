# S. 3220

> To expand the tropical disease product priority review voucher program to encourage prevention and treatment of coccidioidomycosis.

## § 1. Expanding the tropical disease product priority review voucher program to encourage prevention and treatment of coccidioidomycosis

Section 524(a)(3) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (21 U.S.C. 360n(a)(3)) is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating subparagraph (S) as subparagraph (T); and
- **(2)** by inserting after subparagraph (R) the following:
  > - **(S)** Coccidioidomycosis.
