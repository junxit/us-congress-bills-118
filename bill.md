# H.R. 9278

> To amend section 5336 of title 31, United States Code, to provide existing small businesses with an additional year to file beneficial ownership information, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Small Business beneficial ownership reporting extension

Section 5336(b)(1)(B) of title 31, United States Code, is amended by inserting after “paragraph (2)” the following: “, except that any such reporting company that is a small business concern (as defined under section 3 of the Small Business Act (15 U.S.C. 632)) shall submit to FinCEN a report that contains such information during the period beginning on the effective date of the regulations prescribed under this subsection and ending on December 31, 2025”.
