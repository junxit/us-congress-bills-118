# H.R. 5755

> To repeal the Local Rent Supplement Program Eligibility Temporary Amendment Act of 2023.

## § 1. Repeal of Local Rent Supplement Program Eligibility Temporary Amendment Act of 2023

The Local Rent Supplement Program Eligibility Temporary Amendment Act of 2023 (D.C. Law L2–0057) is hereby repealed, and any provision of law amended or repealed by such Act is restored or revived as if such Act had not been enacted into law.
