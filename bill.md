# S. 5140

> To extend by 5 years the term of the Special Inspector General for Pandemic Recovery.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “COVID Spending Transparency Act of 2024”.

## § 2. Termination

Section 4018(h) of the CARES Act (15 U.S.C. 9053(h)) is amended by striking “5” and inserting “10”.
