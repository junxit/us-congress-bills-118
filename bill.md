# H.J. RES. 152

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to “Hazardous and Solid Waste Management System: Disposal of Coal Combustion Residuals From Electric Utilities; Legacy CCR Surface Impoundments”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to “Hazardous and Solid Waste Management System: Disposal of Coal Combustion Residuals From Electric Utilities; Legacy CCR Surface Impoundments” (89 Fed. Reg. 38950 (May 8, 2024)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
