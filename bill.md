# H.J. RES. 224

> Relating to the disapproval of the Presidential report with respect to the indebtedness of the Government of Ukraine.

That Congress disapproves the proposal relating to the indebtedness of the Government of Ukraine submitted by the President of the United States to Congress on November 18, 2024.
