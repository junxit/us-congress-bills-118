# H.J. RES. 181

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Department of Labor relating to “Definition of “Employer”-Association Health Plans”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Department of Labor relating to “Definition of “Employer”—Association Health Plans” (89 Fed. Reg. 34106 (April 30, 2024)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
