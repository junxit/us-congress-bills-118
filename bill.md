# H.R. 8782

> To extend the obligation deadline of funds made available to recipients under the American Rescue Plan Act for the purposes of supporting homeless children and youth.

## § 1. Supporting homeless children and youth

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, funds made available to entities under section 2001(b)(1) of Public Law 117–2 shall remain available to those entities for purposes authorized under such section through September 30, 2025.
