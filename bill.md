# H.R. 1095

> To declare an AR–15 style rifle chambered in a .223 Remington round or a 5.56x45mm NATO round to be the National Gun of the United States.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “AR–15 National Gun Act”.

## § 2. Declaration

An AR–15 style rifle chambered in a .223 Remington round or a 5.56x45mm NATO round is hereby declared to be the National Gun of the United States.
