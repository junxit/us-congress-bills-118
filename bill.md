# H.R. 8670

> To amend the Immigration and Nationality Act to modify the time at which age is assessed for the purpose of determining whether an alien is an immediate relative.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Child Status Fairness and Modernization Act”.

## § 2. Age requirement

Section 201(f)(2) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1151(f)(2)) is amended—

- **(1)** by amending the heading to read as follows: “Age requirement in case of parent naturalization.—”; and
- **(2)** by striking “age of the alien on the date of the parent’s naturalization” and inserting “the date on which the petition was initially filed”.
