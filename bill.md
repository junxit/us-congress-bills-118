# H.R. 8854

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to authorize the provision of certain additional burial benefits for individuals for whom an urn or plaque is furnished, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Ensuring Veterans’ Final Resting Place Act of 2024”.

## § 2. Department of Veterans Affairs provision of additional burial benefits when cost of urn or plaque is reimbursed

Section 2306(h) of title 38, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (2), by striking “If the Secretary” and inserting “Except as provided in paragraph (3), if the Secretary”;
- **(2)** by redesignating paragraphs (3) through (5) as paragraphs (4) through (6), respectively; and
- **(3)** by inserting after paragraph (2) the following new paragraph (3):
  > - **(3)** Paragraph (2) shall not apply with respect to an urn or plaque furnished under paragraph (1) on or after January 5, 2021, if a non-Department entity reimburses the Secretary for the cost of the urn or plaque.
