# H.R. 6932

> To amend the National Dam Safety Program Act to require the inclusion of low-head dams in the national dam inventory, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Low-Head Dam Inventory Act”.

## § 2. National dam inventory

The National Dam Safety Program Act (33 U.S.C. 467d) is amended—

- **(1)** in section 6—
  - **(A)** in the first sentence, by inserting “and low-head dams (as defined in section 15(a))” before “in the United States”; and
  - **(B)** in the second sentence—
    - **(i)** by inserting “and low-head dams” after “Such inventory of dams”;
    - **(ii)** by inserting “and low-head dam” after “each dam”; and
    - **(iii)** by striking “or a State dam safety agency” and inserting “, a State dam safety agency, or another relevant entity”; and
- **(2)** in section 15(b)(2)—
  - **(A)** in subparagraph (A), by striking “and” at the end;
  - **(B)** in subparagraph (B), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and
  - **(C)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(C)** incorporate the data collected on low-head dams into the National Dam Inventory described in section 6.
