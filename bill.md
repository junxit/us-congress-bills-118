# S. 827

> To provide that no Federal funds may be used to implement, administer, or enforce the rule of the Department of Justice entitled “Factoring Criteria for Firearms with Attached “Stabilizing Braces””.

## § 1. No Federal funds for Department of Justice rule on stabilizing braces

No Federal funds may be used to implement, administer, or enforce the rule of the Department of Justice entitled “Factoring Criteria for Firearms with Attached “Stabilizing Braces””.
