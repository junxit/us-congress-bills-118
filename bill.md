# H.R. 7399

> To terminate the American Community Survey, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “American Community Survey Elimination Act” or the “ACSE Act”.

## § 2. Termination of American Community Survey

The Secretary of Commerce or the Bureau of the Census may not carry out the American Community Survey, and no Federal funds may be obligated or expended to carry out such Survey (or any similar survey).
