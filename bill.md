# H.R. 2970

> To amend the Small Business Act to eliminate certain requirements relating to the award of construction subcontracts within the county or State of performance.

## § 1. Elimination of requirement relating to award of construction subcontracts within county or State of performance

Section 8(a) of the Small Business Act (15 U.S.C. 637(a)) is amended by striking paragraph (11).
