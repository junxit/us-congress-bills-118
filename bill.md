# H.R. 1870

> To provide for a limitation on availability of funds for House of Representatives, Office of the Majority Leader for fiscal year 2024.

## § 1. Limitation on availability of funds for House of Representatives, Office of the Majority Leader for fiscal year 2024

Notwithstanding any other provision of the law, amounts authorized to be appropriated or otherwise available for House of Representatives, Office of the Majority Leader for fiscal year 2024 may not exceed $2,642,739.
