# H.R. 7964

> To prohibit the use of Federal funds to establish, implement, or enforce any COVID–19 mask mandate.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Defunding Unscientific Mask Mandates Act of 2024”.

## § 2. No Federal funds To enforce COVID–19 mask mandates

No Federal funds may be used to establish, implement, or enforce any requirement to wear a facial covering to prevent the spread of COVID–19.
