# S. 5142

> To amend the Public Health Service Act with respect to the national program for traumatic brain injury surveillance and registries.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Congressman Bill Pascrell, Jr. Traumatic Brain Injury Program Renaming Act”.

## § 2. The Bill Pascrell, Jr., national program for traumatic brain injury surveillance and registries

Section 393C of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 280b–1d) is amended—

- **(1)** by amending the section heading to read as follows: “The Bill Pascrell, Jr., national program for traumatic brain injury surveillance and registries”;
- **(2)** by striking subsection (b); and
- **(3)** by redesignating subsection (c) as subsection (b).
