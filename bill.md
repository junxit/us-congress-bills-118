# H.R. 9910

> To amend title 5, United States Code, to increase the amount of military leave provided to Federal employees, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Supporting Federal Employees in the National Guard and Reserves Act”.

## § 2. Increase in military leave for Federal employees

Section 6323(a)(1) of title 5, United States Code, is amended by striking “rate of 15 days” and inserting “rate of 36 days”.
