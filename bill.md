# H.J. RES. 126

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation relating to “Principles for Climate-Related Financial Risk Management for Large Financial Institutions”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation relating to “Principles for Climate-Related Financial Risk Management for Large Financial Institutions” (88 Fed. Reg. 74183; published October 30, 2023), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
