# S. 3565

> To appropriate funds for the Affordable Connectivity Program of the Federal Communications Commission.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Affordable Connectivity Program Extension Act of 2024”.

## § 2. Appropriation for Affordable Connectivity Program

Section 904(i)(2) of division N of the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2021 (47 U.S.C. 1752(i)(2)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “There is” and inserting the following:
  > - **(A)** *Fiscal year 2021* There is

- **(2)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(B)** *Fiscal year 2024* There is appropriated to the Affordable Connectivity Fund, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, $7,000,000,000 for fiscal year 2024, to remain available until expended.
