# H.R. 714

> To amend the Agriculture Improvement Act of 2018 to extend the feral swine eradication and control pilot program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Feral Swine Act”.

## § 2. Extension of feral swine eradication and control pilot program

Section 2408(g)(1) of the Agriculture Improvement Act of 2018 (7 U.S.C. 8351 note; Public Law 115–334) is amended by striking “2023” and inserting “2028”.
