# H.R. 9463

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to require that non-citizen appointees to positions in the Veterans Health Administration are subjected to background investigations prior to employment.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Recruitment Improvement for Service Excellence at VA Hospitals Act” or the “RISE Act”.

## § 2. Requirement that non-citizen appointees in Veterans Health Administration are subjected to background investigations prior to employment

Section 7407(a) of title 38, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by inserting (1) before “When”; and
- **(2)** by adding at the end the following new paragraph:
  > - **(2)** A person may not be appointed under paragraph (1) unless the person has been subjected to a background investigation.
