# H.R. 9288

> To direct the Secretary of Defense to procure cleaning products that are identified by the Safer Choice program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Prioritizing PFAS-Free Cleaning Products Act”.

## § 2. Procurement of cleaning products

The Secretary of Defense shall, to the maximum extent practicable, only procure cleaning products that are identified by—

- **(1)** the Safer Choice program; or
- **(2)** an independent third-party organization that provides certifications in a manner consistent with the Safer Choice program.
