# H.J. RES. 180

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Department of Energy relating to “Clean Energy for New Federal Buildings and Major Renovations of Federal Buildings”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Department of Energy relating to “Clean Energy for New Federal Buildings and Major Renovations of Federal Buildings” (89 Fed. Reg. 35384 (May 1, 2024)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
