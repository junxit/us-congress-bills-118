# H.R. 4098

> To amend the Public Health Service Act to reauthorize grants for building communities of recovery.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Communities of Recovery Reauthorization Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Building communities of recovery

Section 547(f) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 290ee–2(f)) is amended by striking “$5,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2019 through 2023” and inserting “$16,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2024 through 2028”.
