# H.R. 1857

> To provide for a limitation on availability of funds for Senate, Office of the Sergeant at Arms and Doorkeeper for fiscal year 2024.

## § 1. Limitation on availability of funds for Senate, Office of the Sergeant at Arms and Doorkeeper for fiscal year 2024

Notwithstanding any other provision of the law, amounts authorized to be appropriated or otherwise available for Senate, Office of the Sergeant at Arms and Doorkeeper for fiscal year 2024 may not exceed $84,157,000.
