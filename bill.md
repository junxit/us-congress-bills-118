# H.J. RES. 115

> Disapproving the rule submitted by the Department of Agriculture relating to “Importation of Fresh Beef From Paraguay”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Department of Agriculture relating to “Importation of Fresh Beef From Paraguay” (88 Fed. Reg. 77883), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
