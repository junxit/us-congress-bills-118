# H.R. 4230

> To transfer the unobligated balances of amounts made available to the Internal Revenue Service under the Inflation Reduction Act to the Secretary of Homeland Security to secure the border.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “The Fund Our Border Not The IRS Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Unobligated balances of the Internal Revenue Service made available to secure the border

The unobligated balances of all amounts made available to the Internal Revenue Service under Public Law 117–169 shall be transferred to the Secretary of Homeland Security and be made available for the salaries and expenses of agents and officers of U.S. Customs and Border Protection, constructing and maintaining physical barriers along the southern international border of the United States, and other purposes relating to border security.
