# H.R. 1293

> To improve the safety of the air supply on aircraft, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Cabin Air Safety Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Improvements to the safety of the air supply on aircraft

- **(a)** *In general* Subpart III of part A of subtitle VII of title 49, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following new chapter:
- **(b)** *Conforming amendments*
  - **(1)** *Table of contents* The table of contents of subpart III of part A of subtitle VII of title 49, United States Code, is amended by inserting after the item relating to chapter 453 the following:
  - **(2)** *Conforming repeal* Section 326 of the FAA Reauthorization Act of 2018 (49 U.S.C. 40101 note) and the item relating to that section in the table of contents under section 1(b) of that Act are repealed.
