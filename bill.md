# S. 3070

> To amend the SUPPORT for Patients and Communities Act to reauthorize the youth prevention and recovery initiative.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Youth Prevention and Recovery Reauthorization Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of youth prevention and recovery initiative

Section 7102(c)(9) of the SUPPORT for Patients and Communities Act (42 U.S.C. 290bb–7a(c)(9)) is amended by striking “2019 through 2023” and inserting “2024 through 2028”.
