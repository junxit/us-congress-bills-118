# H. RES. 930

> Condemning the Cable News Network (CNN) for utilizing images from an imbedded photojournalist with prior knowledge of the October 7th attack on Israel.

## Preamble

- Whereas the New York Post article titled “Netanyahu slams Hamas-linked journos used by CNN, NYT, Reuters and AP who were at Oct. 7 massacre” by Olivia Land and Megan Palin published on November 9th, 2023, exposed the imbedded photojournalist;
- Whereas Prime Minister of Israel Benjamin Netanyahu accused the photojournalists of being “accomplices in crimes against humanity”; and
- Whereas the October 7th massacre in Israel by the Iranian puppet terrorist group Hamas slaughtered innocent civilians including the targeting of women and children including those of American nationality: Now, therefore, be it

That the House of Representatives—

- **(1)** condemns the use of images captured by photojournalists with advance knowledge of the October 7th attack against Israel; and
- **(2)** reaffirms that the House stands against complicity in attacks against civilians.
