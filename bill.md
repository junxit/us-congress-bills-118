# H.J. RES. 119

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Securities and Exchange Commission relating to “Private Fund Advisers; Documentation of Registered Investment Adviser Compliance Reviews”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Securities and Exchange Commission relating to “Private Fund Advisers; Documentation of Registered Investment Adviser Compliance Reviews” (88 Fed. Reg. 63206; published September 14, 2023), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
