# H.R. 1926

> To provide for a limitation on availability of funds for Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration, Ex-Offender Activities for fiscal year 2024.

## § 1. Limitation on availability of funds for Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration, Ex-Offender Activities for fiscal year 2024

Notwithstanding any other provision of the law, amounts authorized to be appropriated or otherwise available for Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration, Ex-Offender Activities for fiscal year 2024 may not exceed $93,079,000.
