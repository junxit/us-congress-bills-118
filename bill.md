# H.R. 5161

> To amend the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 with respect to prohibited uses of funds under such Act, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Protecting Hunting and Archery in Schools Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Prohibited uses of funds

Section 8526 of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 7906) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (5), by adding “or” at the end;
- **(2)** in paragraph (6), by striking “; or” at the end and inserting a period; and
- **(3)** by striking paragraph (7).
