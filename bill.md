# H.J. RES. 111

> Disapproving the rule submitted by the Department of Energy relating to “Energy Conservation Program: Energy Conservation Standards for Consumer Furnaces”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Department of Energy relating to “Energy Conservation Program: Energy Conservation Standards for Consumer Furnaces” (88 Fed. Reg. 87502), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
