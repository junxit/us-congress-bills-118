# H.R. 5139

> To amend the Richard B. Russell National School Lunch Act to increase the fine for certain fraudulent activities related to funds for child nutrition programs, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fighting Their Fraud Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Prevention of fraud under child nutrition programs

Section 12(g) of the Richard B. Russell National School Lunch Act (42 U.S.C. 1760(g)) is amended by striking “$25,000” and inserting “$50,000”.
