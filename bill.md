# H.R. 7304

> To amend title II of the Social Security Act to establish that benefits paid to a parent caring for the child of an individual who died while performing active duty as a member of the Armed Services are not subject to deductions under the retirement earnings test.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Protecting the Families of Our Fallen Patriots Act”.

## § 2. Exemption from retirement earnings test

- **(a)** *In general* Section 203(b) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 403(b)) is amended by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(3)** In any case in which an surviving spouse or surviving divorced parent is entitled to a mother’s or father’s insurance benefit under section 202(g) and such benefit is based on the wages and self-employment income of a deceased individual who died while performing active duty as a member of the Armed Forces, the benefit to which such surviving spouse or surviving divorced parent is entitled on the basis of the wages and self-employment income of such deceased individual shall not be subject to deductions under this subsection.

- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply with respect to benefits payable for months that begin on or after the date of enactment of this Act.
