# H.R. 9312

> To improve passenger vessel security and safety, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title; references

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Cruise Passenger Protection Act of 2024”.
- **(b)** *References to title 46, United States Code* Except as otherwise expressly provided, wherever in this Act an amendment or repeal is expressed in terms of an amendment to, or repeal of, a section or other provision, the reference shall be considered to be made to a section or other provision of title 46, United States Code.
