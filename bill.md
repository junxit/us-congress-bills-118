# H.R. 523

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to increase the authorization of appropriations for homeless veterans reintegration programs.

## § 1. Increase of authorization of appropriations for homeless veterans reintegration programs

Section 2021(i)(1)(G) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “$60,000,000” and inserting “$75,000,000”.
