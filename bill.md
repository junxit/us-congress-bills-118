# H.J. RES. 74

> Relating to a national emergency declared by the President on May 16, 2012.

That, pursuant to section 202 of the National Emergencies Act (50 U.S.C. 1622), the national emergency declared by the finding of the President on May 16, 2012, in Executive Order 13611 is hereby terminated.
