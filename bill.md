# H.R. 7522

> To amend the America’s Conservation Enhancement Act to reauthorize the Chesapeake WILD program.

## § 1. Reauthorization of Chesapeake WILD program

Section 111(e)(1) of the America’s Conservation Enhancement Act (33 U.S.C. 1267 note) is amended by striking “2025” and inserting “2030”.
