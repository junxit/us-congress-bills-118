# H.R. 9387

> To authorize a court to award restitution for unlawful entry and presence in the United States, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Allocating Liability to Illegal Entrants in National Courts Act” or the “ALIEN Act”.

## § 2. Order of restitution for unlawful entry and presence in the United States

Section 3663(a) of title 18, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “or section 5124” and inserting “section 5124”; and
- **(2)** by inserting “or section 275(a) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1325(a)),” before “other than an offense”.
