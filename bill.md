# H. CON. RES. 93

> Providing for a joint session of Congress to receive a message from the President.

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Thursday, March 7, 2024, at 9 p.m., for the purpose of receiving such communication as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.
