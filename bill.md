# H.R. 5199

> To amend the Agricultural Research, Extension, and Education Reform Act of 1998 to reauthorize the Specialty Crop Research Initiative, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Specialty Crop Research Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of Specialty Crop Research Initiative

Section 412 of the Agricultural Research, Extension, and Education Reform Act of 1998 (7 U.S.C. 7632) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (j), by striking paragraph (5); and
- **(2)** in subsection (k), by amending paragraph (1) to read as follows:
  > - **(1)** *Mandatory funding* Of the funds of the Commodity Credit Corporation, the Secretary shall make available to carry out this section—
  >   - **(A)** $80,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2014 through 2023; and
  >   - **(B)** $130,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2024 through 2028.

## § 3. Funding for Inter-Regional Research Project Number 4

Subsection (e) of the Competitive, Special, and Facilities Research Grant Act (7 U.S.C. 3157(e)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (7), by inserting “$50,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2024 through 2028,” after “1991,”; and
- **(2)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(8)** Of the funds of the Commodity Credit Corporation, the Secretary shall make available to carry out this subsection $50,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2024 through 2028.
