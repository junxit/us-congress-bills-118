# S. 4898

> To amend the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act to include extreme heat in the definition of a major disaster.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Extreme Heat Emergency Act of 2024”.

## § 2. Definition of major disaster

Section 102(2) of the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act (42 U.S.C. 5122(2)) is amended by inserting “extreme heat,” before “or drought”.
