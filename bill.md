# S. 1520

> To prohibit the use of Federal funds to purchase, procure, or distribute pipes or cylindrical objects intended to be used to smoke or inhale illegal scheduled substances.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Cutting Rampant Access to Crack Kits Act of 2023” or the “CRACK Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Prohibition on use of Federal funds for pipes intended to be used to smoke or inhale illegal scheduled substances

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, Federal funds made available for the treatment of substance use disorders shall not be used to purchase, procure, or distribute pipes or cylindrical objects intended to be used to smoke or inhale illegal scheduled substances.
