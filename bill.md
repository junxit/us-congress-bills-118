# H.R. 3631

> To amend the Public Health Service Act to extend the authorization of appropriations for grants for State strategic stockpiles.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “State Strategic Stockpile Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of pilot program to support State medical stockpiles

Section 319F–2(i)(9) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 247d–6b(i)(9)) is amended by striking “2024” and inserting “2028”.
