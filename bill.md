# H.R. 719

> To amend the Labor Management Relations Act to prohibit neutrality agreements, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Prohibition on neutrality agreements

Section 302 of the Labor Management Relations Act (29 U.S.C. 186) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a), in the matter preceding paragraph (1), by striking “or deliver” each place it appears and inserting “provide, or deliver”; and
- **(2)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(h)** As used in this section, the term thing of value includes organizing assistance.
