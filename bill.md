# S. 97

> To establish procedures for the removal of the Architect of the Capitol.

## § 1. Removal of the Architect of the Capitol

Section 319 of the Legislative Branch Appropriations Act, 1990 (2 U.S.C. 1801) is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating subsection (b) as subsection (c); and
- **(2)** by inserting after subsection (a) the following:
  > - **(b)** *Removal* The Architect of the Capitol may be removed at any time by—
  >   - **(1)** impeachment; or
  >   - **(2)** joint resolution of Congress, only for—
  >     - **(A)** permanent disability;
  >     - **(B)** inefficiency;
  >     - **(C)** neglect of duty;
  >     - **(D)** malfeasance; or
  >     - **(E)** a felony or conduct involving moral turpitude.
