# H.R. 6310

> To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to extend incentive payments for participation in eligible alternative payment models under the Medicare program.

## § 1. Extending incentive payments for participation in eligible alternative payment models under the Medicare program

Section 1833(z) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395l(z)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (1)(A)—
  - **(A)** by striking “and ending with 2025” and inserting “and ending with 2027”; and
  - **(B)** by striking “with respect to 2025” and inserting “with respect to each of years 2025 through 2027”; and
- **(2)** in paragraph (4), by striking “with respect to 2025” and inserting “with respect to each of years 2025 through 2027”.
