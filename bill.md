# S. 2218

> To authorize the appropriation of amounts for the construction of a battle management combined operations complex at Robins Air Force Base, Georgia.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Robins Air Force Base Battle Management Enhancement Act”.

## § 2. Authorization of amounts for construction of battle management combined operations complex at Robins Air Force Base, Georgia

There is authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary of Defense for fiscal year 2024 $115,000,000 for the construction of a battle management combined operations complex at Robins Air Force Base, Georgia.
