# S. 3822

> To designate the medical center of the Department of Veterans Affairs in West Palm Beach, Florida, as the “Thomas H. Corey VA Medical Center”.

## § 1. Designation of Thomas H. Corey VA Medical Center in West Palm Beach, Florida

- **(a)** *In general* The medical center of the Department of Veterans Affairs in West Palm Beach, Florida, shall after the date of the enactment of this Act be known and designated as the “Thomas H. Corey VA Medical Center”.
- **(b)** *Reference* Any reference in any law, regulation, map, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the medical center referred to in subsection (a) shall be considered to be a reference to the Thomas H. Corey VA Medical Center.
