# S. 4468

> To prohibit the use of Federal funds to finalize, implement, or enforce the interim final rule of the Bureau of Industry and Security of the Department of Commerce entitled “Revision of Firearms License Requirements”.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Stop the Bureaucratic Ineptitude Shuttering Respectable and Upstanding Lawful Exporters Act” or the “Stop the BIS RULE Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on use of Federal funds to finalize, implement, or enforce proposed rule of Bureau of Industry and Security

No Federal funds may be obligated or expended to finalize, implement, or enforce the interim final rule of the Bureau of Industry and Security of the Department of Commerce entitled “Revision of Firearms License Requirements” and published in the Federal Register on April 30, 2024 (Docket No. 240419–0113; 89 Fed. Reg. 34680).
