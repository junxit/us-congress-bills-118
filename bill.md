# S. 3046

> To make permanent the authority to collect Shasta-Trinity National Forest marina fees.

## § 1. Permanent authorization to collect Shasta-Trinity National Forest marina fees

Section 422 of the Department of the Interior, Environment, and Related Agencies Appropriations Act, 2008 (Public Law 110–161; 121 Stat. 2149; 123 Stat. 747; 128 Stat. 346), is amended by striking “through fiscal year 2019”.
