# S. 4922

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to extend the authority for transportation of individuals to and from facilities of the Department of Veterans Affairs.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “VA Transportation Programs Act of 2024”.

## § 2. Extension of authority for transportation of individuals to and from facilities of Department of Veterans Affairs

Section 111A(a)(2) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “September 30, 2024” and inserting “September 30, 2029”.
