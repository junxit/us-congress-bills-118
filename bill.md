# H.R. 8060

> To amend the Immigration and Nationality Act to require the notification of appropriate elected officials prior to the placement of refugees in a State.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Contacting All Local Legislators Act” or the “CALL Act”.

## § 2. Notification of elected officials prior to refugee resettlement

Section 412(a)(2)(D) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1522(a)(2)(D)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “shall, consistent” and inserting the following:
  > shall—
  > - **(i)** consistent

- **(2)** by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(ii)** prior to making such a placement, notify the Senators representing the State, the Member of the House of Representatives representing the district of the placement within the State, and the applicable State legislative elected officials.
