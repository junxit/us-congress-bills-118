# H.R. 6690

> To amend the Commodity Credit Corporation Charter Act to authorize the use of Commodity Credit Corporation funds for emergency assistance to dairy producers in the case of livestock relocation and feed crop losses due to natural disasters, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Funding authority for livestock relocation and feed crop losses due to natural disasters

Section 5 of the Commodity Credit Corporation Charter Act (15 U.S.C. 714c) is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating subsection (h) as subsection (i); and
- **(2)** by inserting after subsection (g) the following:
  > - **(h)** Provide emergency assistance to dairy and livestock producers for livestock relocation, shelter-in-place, and feed crop losses due to natural disasters.
