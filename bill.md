# S.J. RES. 110

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the National Park Service relating to “Alaska; Hunting and Trapping in National Preserves”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the National Park Service relating to “Alaska; Hunting and Trapping in National Preserves” (89 Fed. Reg. 55059 (July 3, 2024)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
