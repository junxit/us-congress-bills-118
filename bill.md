# H.R. 746

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to expand certain rehabilitation programs for certain veterans with service-connected disabilities.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Streamlining Aviation for Eligible Veterans Act” or the “SAFE Veterans Act”.

## § 2. Inclusion of non-degree flight training in certain rehabilitation programs for certain veterans with service-connected disabilities

Section 3101 of title 38, United States Code, is amended in paragraph (6) by adding at the end the following: “A rehabilitation program may include a program that includes flight training and does not lead to a degree.”.
