# H.R. 1858

> To provide for a limitation on availability of funds for Senate, Offices of the Secretaries for the Majority and Minority for fiscal year 2024.

## § 1. Limitation on availability of funds for Senate, Offices of the Secretaries for the Majority and Minority for fiscal year 2024

Notwithstanding any other provision of the law, amounts authorized to be appropriated or otherwise available for Senate, Offices of the Secretaries for the Majority and Minority for fiscal year 2024 may not exceed $1,900,000.
