# S. 5362

> To amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 regarding the application of wage and hour provisions to minor league baseball players, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fair Ball Act”.

## § 2. Application of the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 to minor league baseball players

Section 13(a)(19) of the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 (29 U.S.C. 213(a)(19)) is amended by striking “a contract” and all that follows through the period at the end and inserting “an unexpired collective bargaining agreement.”.
