# S. 4966

> To authorize public housing agencies to use assistance from the Capital Fund for energy performance contracts.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Expanding Energy Efficiency for Affordable Housing Act of 2024”.

## § 2. Use of Capital Fund assistance by public housing agencies for energy performance contracts

Section 9(d) of the United States Housing Act of 1937 (42 U.S.C. 1437g(d)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (1)(K), by inserting before “and by” the following: “by entering into energy performance contracts (as defined in paragraph (4)),”; and
- **(2)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(4)** *Definition of energy performance contract* For purposes of this subsection, the term energy performance contract means a contract described in section 990.185(a) of title 24, Code of Federal Regulations, or any successor regulation.
