# S. 817

> To repeal title IV of the Economic Growth, Regulatory Relief, and Consumer Protection Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Secure Viable Banking Act” or the “SVB Act”.

## § 2. Restoration of banking regulations

Title IV of the Economic Growth, Regulatory Relief, and Consumer Protection Act (Public Law 115–174; 132 Stat. 1296) is repealed, and the provisions of law amended by that title are restored as if that title had not been enacted.
