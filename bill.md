# S. RES. 497

> To express the sense of the Senate that the slogan “From the river to the sea, Palestine will be free” and its derivations are antisemitic and a call for genocide and the destruction of the Jewish state.

## § 1. Sense of the Senate regarding the meaning of the slogan “From the river to the sea, Palestine will be free”

It is the sense of the Senate that the slogan “From the river to the sea, Palestine will be free” and its derivations are antisemitic and a call for genocide and the destruction of the Jewish state.
