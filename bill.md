# S. 2219

> To amend the Congressional Accountability Act of 1995 to expand access to breastfeeding accommodations in the workplace.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “PUMP Technical Correction Act”.

## § 2. Breastfeeding accommodation coverage for certain congressional employees

Section 203(a)(1) of the Congressional Accountability Act of 1995 (2 U.S.C. 1313(a)(1)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “and section 12(c)” and inserting “section 12(c), and section 18D”; and
- **(2)** by inserting “, 218d” after “212(c)”.
