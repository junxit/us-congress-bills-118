# H.R. 3027

> To reauthorize funding for the Reclamation Climate Change and Water Program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Reclamation Climate Change and Water Program Reauthorization Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Reclamation Climate Change and Water Program

Section 9503(f) of the Omnibus Public Land Management Act of 2009 (42 U.S.C. 10363(f)) is amended by striking “2023” and inserting “2033”.
