# H.R. 3375

> To establish programs to address addiction and overdoses caused by illicit fentanyl and other opioids, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title; table of contents

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Support, Treatment, and Overdoses Prevention of Fentanyl Overdoses Act of 2023” or the “STOP Fentanyl Overdoses Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Definitions

In this Act, except as otherwise provided:

- **(1)** The term Assistant Secretary means the Assistant Secretary for Mental Health and Substance Use.
- **(2)** The term Secretary means the Secretary of Health and Human Services.
- **(3)** The term fentanyl-related substance has the meaning given the term in section 1308.11(h)(30)(i) of title 21, Code of Federal Regulations (or successor regulations).
