# S. 5207

> To amend the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act to modify certain requirements relating to emergency loans, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Emergency Loans Reform Act of 2024”.

## § 2. Factors for consideration in making loan determinations

Section 322 of the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (7 U.S.C. 1962) is amended—

  > ## § 322. Factors for consideration in making loan determinations

  > For

- **(2)** by striking subsection (b).
