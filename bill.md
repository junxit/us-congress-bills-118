# H.R. 2359

> To provide for a limitation on availability of funds for Contribution to the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development for fiscal year 2024.

## § 1. Limitation on availability of funds for Contribution to the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development for fiscal year 2024

Notwithstanding any other provision of the law, amounts authorized to be appropriated or otherwise available for Contribution to the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development for fiscal year 2024 may not exceed $0.
