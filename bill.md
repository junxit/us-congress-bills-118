# H.R. 337

> To prohibit Federal agencies from banning gas-burning stoves.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Guarding America’s Stoves Act” or the “GAS Act”.

## § 2. Agency prohibition on banning gas stoves

- **(a)** *In general* No agency may prohibit the use of or sale of gas-burning stoves in the United States.
- **(b)** *Agency defined* In this section, the term agency has the meaning given that term in section 551 of title 5, United States Code.
