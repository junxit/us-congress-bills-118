# S. 4554

> To express support for protecting access to reproductive health care after the Dobbs v. Jackson decision on June 24, 2022.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Reproductive Freedom for Women Act”.

## § 2. Sense of Congress

It is the sense of Congress that—

- **(1)** protections for access to abortion rights and other reproductive health care after the Dobbs v. Jackson, 597 U.S. 215 (2022) decision on June 24, 2022, should be supported; and
- **(2)** the protections enshrined in Roe v. Wade, 410 U.S. 113 (1973) should be restored and built upon, moving towards a future where there is reproductive freedom for all.
