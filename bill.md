# H.R. 1921

> To provide for a limitation on availability of funds for Library of Congress, John C. Stennis Center for Public Service Training and Development for fiscal year 2024.

## § 1. Limitation on availability of funds for Library of Congress, John C. Stennis Center for Public Service Training and Development for fiscal year 2024

Notwithstanding any other provision of the law, amounts authorized to be appropriated or otherwise available for Library of Congress, John C. Stennis Center for Public Service Training and Development for fiscal year 2024 may not exceed $430,000.
