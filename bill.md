# H. CON. RES. 48

> Condemning the Chinese Communist Party for the Wuhan Lab “leak” causing the deadly COVID–19 pandemic.

That Congress condemns the Chinese Communist Party as responsible for—

- **(1)** the Wuhan Lab “leak” causing the deadly COVID–19 pandemic to damage the United States economy, national security, and public health; and
- **(2)** the resulting deaths of over 1,000,000 Americans.
