# S. 3929

> To prohibit the Secretary of Agriculture from taking certain proposed actions relating to a land management plan direction for old-growth forest conditions across the National Forest System.

## § 1. Prohibition on taking proposed actions

The Secretary of Agriculture shall not take the proposed actions described in the notice of the Forest Service entitled “Land Management Plan Direction for Old-Growth Forest Conditions Across the National Forest System” (88 Fed. Reg. 88042 (December 20, 2023)).
