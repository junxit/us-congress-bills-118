# H.R. 5627

> To require a report relating to the Special Presidential Envoy for Climate.

## § 1. Special Presidential Envoy for Climate reporting requirements

- **(a)** *In general* Not later than 90 days after the date of the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of State shall submit to the appropriate congressional committees a report on the following:
  - **(1)** Budget information detailing expenditures of the Special Presidential Envoy for Climate, during fiscal years 2021 and 2022.
  - **(2)** The number of temporary and full-time employees currently working in the Office of the Special Presidential Envoy for Climate, including the names of all such full-time employees.
  - **(3)** Information on the Special Presidential Envoy for Climate’s foreign travel, including—
    - **(A)** carbon emissions from air travel, as required by Executive Order 14057; and
    - **(B)** the dates, attendees, and discussion topics from all meetings with foreign leaders.
- **(b)** *Definitions* In this section—
  - **(1)** the term “appropriate congressional committees” means the Committee on Foreign Affairs of the House of Representatives and the Committee on Foreign Relations of the Senate; and
  - **(2)** the term “Office of the Special Presidential Envoy for Climate” means the staff, full-time employees, temporary employees, and contractors, supporting the Special Presidential Envoy for Climate.
