# H.J. RES. 68

> Relating to a national emergency declared by the President on October 27, 2006.

That, pursuant to section 202 of the National Emergencies Act (50 U.S.C. 1622), the national emergency declared by the finding of the President on October 27, 2006, in Executive Order 13413 is hereby terminated.
