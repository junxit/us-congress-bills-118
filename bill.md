# S. 4244

> To amend the Community Development Banking and Financial Institutions Act of 1994 to include in technical assistance provided by the Community Development Financial Institutions Fund resources for non-English speaking individuals, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Community Finance Language Inclusion Act of 2024”.

## § 2. Inclusion of resources for non-English speaking individuals in technical assistance provided by the Community Development Financial Institutions Fund

Section 108(c)(1) of the Community Development Banking and Financial Institutions Act of 1994 (12 U.S.C. 4707(c)(1)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “training” and inserting “the training”;
- **(2)** by striking “and development” and inserting “, the development”; and
- **(3)** by striking “products.” and inserting “products, and the provision of outreach and resources for non-English speaking individuals or individuals with limited English proficiency.”.
