# H.R. 8336

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to establish a counseling pathway in the Transition Assistance Program for members of the reserve components of the Armed Forces.

## § 1. Establishment of counseling pathway in the Transition Assistance Program for members of the reserve components of the Armed Forces

Section 1142(c)(1) of title 10, United States Code, is amended, in the matter preceding subparagraph (A), by inserting “(including one pathway for members of the reserve components)” after “military department concerned”.
