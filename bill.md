# S. 5626

> To modify the Federal share of operating costs for certain projects that receive grants under the Formula Grants to Rural Areas Program of the Federal Transit Administration.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Investments in Rural Transit Act of 2024”.

## § 2. Increased Federal share of operating costs for certain areas

Section 5311(g)(2) of title 49, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by striking in “Assistance.—” and all that follows through “subparagraph (B), a” and inserting “Assistance.—A”;
- **(2)** by striking “50 percent” and inserting “80 percent”; and
- **(3)** by striking subparagraph (B).
