# H.R. 7997

> To amend the Trade Act of 1974 to provide for the automatic exclusion of China as a beneficiary country.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Safeguarding Trade Opportunities and Preventing China’s Anti-Competitive Practices Act”.

## § 2. Expansion of automatic exclusion

Section 502 of the Trade Act of 1974 (19 U.S.C. 2462) is amended in subsection (b)(1) by adding at the end the following new subparagraph:

> - **(J)** China.
