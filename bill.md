# H.R. 6158

> To amend the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 to authorize the acquisition and installation of gunshot detection systems under the matching grant program for school security.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “21st Century School Protection Act”.

## § 2. Acquisition and installation of gunshot detection systems under the matching grant program for school security

Section 2701(b) of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 (34 U.S.C. 10551(b)) is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating paragraph (9) as paragraph (10); and
- **(2)** by inserting after paragraph (8) the following:
  > - **(9)** Acquisition and installation of gunshot detection systems.
