# H.J. RES. 173

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Department of Energy relating to “Energy Conservation Program: Energy Conservation Standards for Consumer Water Heaters”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Department of Energy relating to “Energy Conservation Program: Energy Conservation Standards for Consumer Water Heaters” (89 Fed. Reg. 37778 (May 6, 2024)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
