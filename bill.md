# S. 5489

> To amend the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act to adjust the definition of “small impoverished community”.

## § 1. Small impoverished community definition

Section 203(a) of the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act (42 U.S.C. 5133(a)) is amended by striking “3,000” and inserting “5,000”.
