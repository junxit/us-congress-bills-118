# H.J. RES. 153

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Federal Communications Commission relating to “Safeguarding and Securing the Open Internet; Restoring Internet Freedom”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Federal Communications Commission relating to “Safeguarding and Securing the Open Internet; Restoring Internet Freedom” (89 Fed. Reg. 45404 (May 22, 2024)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
