# H.R. 6164

> To prohibit the Secretary of Homeland Security from granting certain aliens temporary protected status.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “No Temporary Protected Status for Terrorists Act” or the “No TPS for Terrorists Act”.

## § 2. Certain aliens ineligible for temporary protected status

- **(a)** *In general* The Secretary of Homeland Security may not grant a covered alien temporary protected status in the United States pursuant to section 244 of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1254a).
- **(b)** *Covered alien* In this section, the term “covered alien” means an alien who is a holder of a passport or other travel document issued by the Palestinian Authority.
