# S. 783

> To require the Energy Information Administration to submit to Congress and make publicly available an annual report on Federal agency policies and regulations and Executive orders that have increased or may increase energy prices in the United States, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Give Americans Stability at Pumps as Rising Inflation Causes Emergencies Act” or the “GAS PRICE Act”.

## § 2. Report on policies, regulations, and Executive orders affecting energy prices

Not later than 60 days after the date of enactment of this Act, and every year thereafter, the Energy Information Administration shall submit to Congress and make publicly available a report detailing each Federal agency policy or regulation and each Executive order with an effective date of January 20, 2021, or later that the Energy Information Administration determines has increased or may increase energy prices in the United States.
