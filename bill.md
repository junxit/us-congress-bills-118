# H.R. 10299

> To amend title XIX of the Social Security Act to prohibit Federal financial participation for gun violence prevention or intervention programs under the Medicaid program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Medicaid Funds Integrity Act of 2024”.

## § 2. Prohibiting Federal financial participation for gun violence prevention or intervention programs under the Medicaid program

Section 1903(i) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1396b(i)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (26), by striking “or” at the end;
- **(2)** in paragraph (27), by striking the period and inserting “; or”; and
- **(3)** by inserting after paragraph (27) the following new paragraph:
  > - **(28)** with respect to any amounts expended for gun violence prevention programs or gun violence intervention programs.
