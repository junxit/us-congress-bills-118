# S.J. RES. 56

> Providing for congressional disapproval of the proposed foreign military sales to the Government of Egypt of certain defense articles and services.

That the following proposed foreign military sales to the Government of Egypt are prohibited:

- **(1)** The sale of the following defense articles and services, described in Transmittal No. 22–58, submitted to Congress pursuant to section 36(b)(1) of the Arms Export Control Act (22 U.S.C. 2776(b)(1)), and published in the Congressional Record on January 10, 2024: 28-Meter Patrol Craft Kits and related equipment.
- **(2)** The sale of the following defense articles and services, described in Transmittal No. 22–54, submitted to Congress pursuant to section 36(b)(1) of the Arms Export Control Act (22 U.S.C. 2776(b)(1)), and published in the Congressional Record on January 10, 2024: Light Tactical Vehicle Chassis and Fleet Build and related equipment.
