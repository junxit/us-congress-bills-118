# S. 2310

> To prohibit agencies from using the term “Latinx” in official communications or forms.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Respect for Hispanic Americans Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on use of “Latinx”

- **(a)** *Agency defined* In this section, the term agency has the meaning given the term in section 551 of title 5, United States Code.
- **(b)** *Prohibition* The head of an agency and any employee of the agency may not use the term Latinx in any official communication or form of the agency.
