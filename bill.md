# S. 3596

> To amend the Mineral Leasing Act to amend references of gilsonite to asphaltite.

## § 1. Mineral amendment to the Mineral Leasing Act

The first section of the Mineral Leasing Act (30 U.S.C. 181) and sections 21, 27, 34, 39, and 43 of that Act (30 U.S.C. 241, 184, 182, 209, 226–3) are amended by striking “gilsonite” each place it appears and inserting “asphaltite”.
