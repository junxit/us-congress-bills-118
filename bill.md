# H.R. 8510

> To amend the Food Security Act of 1985 to encourage the use of native vegetation, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Encouragement of native vegetation use

Section 1244(h) of the Food Security Act of 1985 (16 U.S.C. 3844(h)) is amended—

- **(1)** in the heading, by inserting “and native vegetation use” after “protection”;
- **(2)** in paragraph (1), by striking “; and” and inserting a semicolon;
- **(3)** in paragraph (2), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and
- **(4)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(3)** the use of native vegetation.
