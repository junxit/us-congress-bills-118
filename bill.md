# S. 3887

> To amend title 18, United States Code, to increase the penalty for rioting.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Stop Pro-Terrorist Riots Now Act”.

## § 2. Riots

Section 2101 of title 18, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a), in the matter preceding subsection (b)—
  - **(A)** by striking “subparagraph (A), (B), (C), or (D) of this paragraph—” and inserting “paragraph (1), (2), (3), or (4) of this subsection—”;
  - **(B)** by inserting “less than one year if the defendant, in carrying out an activity described in this subsection, committed an act of violence or aided or abetted any other person in committing an act of violence, nor” before “more”; and
  - **(C)** by striking “five” and inserting “ten”; and
- **(2)** in subsection (b), by striking “subparagraph (A), (B), (C), or (D) of paragraph (1) of subsection (a)” and inserting “paragraph (1), (2), (3), or (4) of subsection (a)”.
