# S. 3454

> To prohibit the use of Federal funds to purchase at-home tests for SARS–CoV–2 from certain foreign entities.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “No Taxpayer Dollars for Communist China COVID Tests Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on using Federal funds to purchase certain tests

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, no Federal funds may be used to purchase any at-home test for SARS–CoV–2 that is—

- **(1)** manufactured, in whole or in part, in the People's Republic of China;
- **(2)** imported from the People's Republic of China; or
- **(3)** manufactured by any entity that is a subsidiary of a Chinese company.
