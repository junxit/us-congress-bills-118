# S. 3585

> To amend title 9, United States Code, with respect to arbitration of disputes involving human trafficking.

## § 1. Arbitration of disputes involving human trafficking

- **(a)** *In general* Chapter 4 of title 9, United States Code, is amended—
  - **(1)** in the heading, by striking “assault and sexual harassment” and inserting “assault, sexual harassment, and human trafficking”;
  - **(2)** in section 401—
    - **(A)** by redesignating paragraphs (1) through (4) as paragraphs (2) through (5), respectively; and
    - **(B)** by inserting before paragraph (2), as so redesignated, the following:
      > - **(1)** *Human trafficking dispute* The term human trafficking dispute means a dispute relating to conduct prohibited by—
      >   - **(A)** chapter 77 of title 18; or
      >   - **(B)** Tribal or State laws prohibiting human trafficking.

  - **(3)** in section 402(a)—
    - **(A)** by striking “dispute or sexual assault dispute” and inserting “dispute, sexual assault dispute, or human trafficking dispute”; and
    - **(B)** by striking “dispute or the sexual harassment dispute” and inserting “dispute, sexual harassment dispute, or human trafficking dispute”.
