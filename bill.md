# H.R. 7006

> To prohibit natural asset companies from entering into any agreement with respect to land in the State of Utah or natural assets on or in such land.

## § 1. Prohibition

- **(a)** *In general* A natural asset company may not enter into any agreement with respect to land in the State of Utah or natural assets on or in such land.
- **(b)** *Natural asset company defined* In this section, the term “natural asset company”—
  - **(1)** has the meaning given that term under the notice of the Securities and Exchange Commission titled “Self-Regulatory Organizations; New York Stock Exchange LLC; Notice of Filing of Proposed Rule Change To Amend the NYSE Listed Company Manual To Adopt Listing Standards for Natural Asset Companies” (88 Fed. Reg. 68811; published October 4, 2023); and
  - **(2)** includes any company that is substantially similar to a company described under paragraph (1).
