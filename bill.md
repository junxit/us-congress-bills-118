# H.R. 8439

> To amend the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 to modify household income to exclude income earned by a member who is 21 years of age or younger and who is an elementary or secondary school student.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “ThinkDIFFERENTLY Nutrition and Opportunities Act of 2024”.

## § 2. Amendment

Section 5(d)(7) of the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 (7 U.S.C. 2014(d)(7)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “a child who is”, and
- **(2)** by striking “17” and inserting “21”.

## § 3. Effective date

This Act and the amendments made by this Act shall take effect on January 1, 2025.
