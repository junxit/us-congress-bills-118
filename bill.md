# H.R. 6267

> To amend title 5, United States Code, to establish Election Day as a Federal holiday.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Election Day Act”.

## § 2. Patriot day

Section 6103(a) of title 5, United States Code, is amended by inserting after the item relating to Columbus Day the following:

> -  Election Day.
