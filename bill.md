# S.J. RES. 78

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Bureau of Land Management relating to “Fluid Mineral Leases and Leasing Process”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Bureau of Land Management relating to “Fluid Mineral Leases and Leasing Process” (89 Fed. Reg. 30916 (April 23, 2024)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
