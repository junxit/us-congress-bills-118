# H. CON. RES. 94

> Directing the Clerk of the House of Representatives to make a correction in the enrollment of H.R. 4366.

That in the enrollment of the bill H.R. 4366, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall make the following correction:

Amend the title so as to read: “Making consolidated appropriations for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2024, and for other purposes.”.
