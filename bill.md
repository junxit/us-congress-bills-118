# H.R. 8379

> To codify certain rules issued by the Secretary of Transportation relating to airline fare transparency, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Enhancing Transparency from Airlines Act of 2024” or the “ETA Act of 2024”.

## § 2. Codification of rules relating to airline fare transparency

The following final rules issued by the Secretary of Transportation shall have the force and effect of law:

- **(1)** The rule titled “Refunds and Other Consumer Protections” and published on April 26, 2024 (89 Fed. Reg. 32760).
- **(2)** The rule titled “Enhancing Transparency of Airline Ancillary Service Fees” and published on April 30, 2024 (89 Fed. Reg. 34620).
