# H.R. 2776

> To abolish the Advisory Committee on Racial Equity of the Department of the Treasury.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Go Woke, Go Broke Act”.

## § 2. Abolishment

The Advisory Committee on Racial Equity of the Department of the Treasury is hereby abolished, and the Secretary of the Treasury may not re-establish such advisory committee or establish any substantially similar advisory committee.
