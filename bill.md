# H.R. 2931

> To amend the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 to provide incentives for States to implement policy changes to reduce prison populations, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Smart Sentencing Adjustments Act”.

## § 2. Grant program

- **(a)** *In general* Title I of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Street Act of 1968 (34 U.S.C. 10101 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following:
- **(b)** *Authorization of appropriations* Section 1001(a) of title I of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 (34 U.S.C. 10261(a)) is amended by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(29)** There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out part PP $2,000,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2024 through 2034.
