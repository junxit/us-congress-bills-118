# H.R. 6289

> To provide that Executive Order 14027 shall have no force or effect.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Stop Climate Hysteria in Diplomacy Act”.

## § 2. Executive Order 14027

Executive Order 14027 (86 Fed. Reg. 25947; relating to establishment of the climate change support office) shall have no force or effect.
