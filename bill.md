# H.R. 3689

> To authorize appropriations for the Minority Serving Institutions Intern Program of the Federal Aviation Administration.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “American Aviation Diversity Act”.

## § 2. Authorization of appropriations

There is authorized to be appropriated $7,500,000 for each fiscal year for the Minority Serving Institutions Intern Program of the Federal Aviation Administration.
