# H.R. 3992

> To amend the Financial Stability Act of 2010 to require stress tests performed pursuant to that Act to evaluate for the increase and decrease of interest rates, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Effective Bank Regulation Act”.

## § 2. Stress test scenario

Section 165(i)(1)(B)(i) of the Financial Stability Act of 2010 (12 U.S.C. 5365(i)(1)(B)(i)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “2 different” and inserting “5 different”; and
- **(2)** by striking “baseline and severely adverse” and inserting “baseline, adverse, severely adverse, a set of conditions that evaluates for increases in interest rates, and a set of conditions that evaluates for decreases in interest rates”.
