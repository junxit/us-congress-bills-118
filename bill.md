# H.R. 2550

> To extend funding for Special Diabetes Programs for Type I diabetes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Special Diabetes Program Reauthorization Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Extension of Special Diabetes Programs for Type I diabetes

Section 330B(b)(2)(D) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 254c–2(b)(2)(D)) is amended by striking “2018 through 2023” and inserting “2018 through 2028”.
