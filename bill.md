# H.J. RES. 147

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Occupational Safety and Health Administration relating to “Worker Walkaround Representative Designation Process”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Occupational Safety and Health Administration relating to “Worker Walkaround Representative Designation Process” (89 Fed. Reg. 22558 (April 1, 2024)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
