# H.J. RES. 197

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Nuclear Regulatory Commission relating to “Fee Schedules; Fee Recovery for Fiscal Year 2024”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Nuclear Regulatory Commission relating to “Fee Schedules; Fee Recovery for Fiscal Year 2024” (89 Fed. Reg. 51789; published June 20, 2024), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
