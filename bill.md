# H.R. 6139

> To direct the Postal Service to establish a post office at 73 West Stewart Road in Lathrop, California, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Establish post office at 73 West Stewart Road in Lathrop, California

- **(a)** *In general* The Postal Service, using the authority under section 401 of title 39, United States Code, shall seek to establish a post office at 73 West Steward Road in Lathrop, California, not later than one year after the date of the enactment of this Act.
- **(b)** *Good faith negotiations* In carrying out subsection (a), the Postal Service shall seek to enter into good faith negotiations with all owners of the location described in such subsection to acquire or lease such location for the purpose of establishing a post office at such location.
- **(c)** *Borrowing limit exception* To the extent necessary to establish a post office at the location described in subsection (a) pursuant to this Act, obligations issued for the costs of establishing such post office shall be disregarded with respect to limitations under section 2005(a).
