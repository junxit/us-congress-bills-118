# H.R. 4710

> To require the Director of the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention to accelerate the collection and publication of data on suicide and drug overdoses.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Publish Overdose and Suicide Tracking For American Safety Today Act of 2023” or the “POST FAST Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Accelerated collection and publication of data on suicide and drug overdoses

The Director of the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention shall—

- **(1)** take such actions as may be necessary to accelerate the collection by the Centers of data on suicide and drug overdoses in the United States; and
- **(2)** not later than 60 days after the end of each month, publish such data for such month.
