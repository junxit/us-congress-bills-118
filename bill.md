# H.R. 7049

> To repeal the Department of Veterans Affairs directive relating to the COVID–19 vaccination program for Veterans Health Administration health care personnel, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Repeal of Department of Veterans Affairs directive relating to COVID–19 vaccination program for certain personnel

The Secretary of Veterans Affairs—

- **(1)** shall repeal Veterans Health Administration Directive 1193.01(1) titled “Coronavirus Disease 2019 Vaccination Program for Veterans Health Administration Health Care Personnel” and published on January 27, 2022 (as amended on December 1, 2022); and
- **(2)** may not issue any substantially similar directive.
