# S. 2104

> To amend the Federal Crop Insurance Act to allow for the separation of enterprise units by continuous and fallow cropping systems.

## § 1. Enterprise units for continuous and fallow cropping systems

Section 508(e)(5) of the Federal Crop Insurance Act (7 U.S.C. 1508(e)(5)) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(F)** *Fallow cropping systems* Beginning with the 2024 crop year, the Corporation shall make available separate enterprise units for fallow and continuous acreage of crops in counties.
