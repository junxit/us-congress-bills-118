# H.R. 2521

> To amend the Safe Drinking Water Act with respect to assistance for disadvantaged communities, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Clean Drinking Water Equity Act”.

## § 2. Assistance for disadvantaged communities

Section 1452(d)(2)(A) of the Safe Drinking Water Act (42 U.S.C. 300j–12(d)(2)(A)) is amended by striking “35 percent” and inserting “40 percent”.
