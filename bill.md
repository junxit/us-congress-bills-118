# H.J. RES. 91

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Department of Energy relating to the “Energy Conservation Program: Test Procedure for Consumer Water Heaters and Residential-Duty Commercial Water Heaters”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Department of Energy relating to the “Energy Conservation Program: Test Procedure for Consumer Water Heaters and Residential-Duty Commercial Water Heaters” (88 Fed. Reg. 40406 (June 21, 2023)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
