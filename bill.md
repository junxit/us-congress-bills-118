# H.R. 6161

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to allow amounts paid for over-the-counter naloxone to be taken into account in determining the deduction for medical expenses.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Supporting Access to Vital and Affordable Care for Everyone Act of 2023” or as the “SAVE Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Amounts paid for over-the-counter naloxone taken into account in determining the deduction for medical expenses

- **(a)** *In general* Section 213(b) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by inserting “or naloxone” after “insulin”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to taxable years ending after the date of the enactment of this Act.
