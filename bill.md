# H.R. 7003

> To amend the National Landslide Preparedness Act to reauthorize such Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “National Landslide Preparedness Act Reauthorization Act of 2024”.

## § 2. National Landslide Preparedness Act reauthorization

The National Landslide Preparedness Act (43 U.S.C. 3101 et seq.) is amended—

- **(1)** in section 3(h) (43 U.S.C. 3102(h)), by striking “2021 through 2024” and inserting “2024 through 2028”; and
- **(2)** in section 5(e), by striking “2021 through 2024” and inserting “2024 through 2028”.
