# H.R. 10229

> To amend the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act to extend the authorization of appropriations for the Clean Energy Demonstration Program on Current and Former Mine Land.

## § 1. Extension of Clean Energy Demonstration Program on Current and Former Mine Land

Section 40342(f) of the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act (42 U.S.C. 18761(f)) is amended by striking “2026” and inserting “2030”.
