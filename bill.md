# H. RES. 1017

> Eliminating the restriction that witnesses may appear remotely at proceedings of committees of the House of Representatives only at the discretion of the chair of the committee.

## § 1. Short title

This resolution may be cited as the “Bringing Real Ideas, Data, and Genuine Experience to Congress Resolution” or the “BRIDGE to Congress Resolution”.

## § 2. Elimination of restriction that witnesses may appear remotely at committee proceedings only at discretion of chair

Section 3(j)(1) of House Resolution 5, agreed to January 9, 2023, is amended by striking “at the discretion of the chair of the committee and”.
