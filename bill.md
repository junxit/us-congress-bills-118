# S. 1061

> To prospectively repeal the 2001 Authorization for Use of Military Force.

## § 1. Prospective repeal of 2001 Authorization for Use of Military Force

Effective on July 20, 2025, the Authorization for Use of Military Force (Public Law 107–40; 115 Stat. 224; 50 U.S.C. 1541 note) is repealed.
