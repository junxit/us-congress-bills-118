# H.J. RES. 70

> Relating to a national emergency declared by the President on February 25, 2011.

That, pursuant to section 202 of the National Emergencies Act (50 U.S.C. 1622), the national emergency declared by the finding of the President on February 25, 2011, in Executive Order 13566 is hereby terminated.
