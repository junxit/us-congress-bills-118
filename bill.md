# H.R. 2096

> To provide for a limitation on availability of funds for District of Columbia, Federal Payment for Defender Services in DC Courts for fiscal year 2024.

## § 1. Limitation on availability of funds for District of Columbia, Federal Payment for Defender Services in DC Courts for fiscal year 2024

Notwithstanding any other provision of the law, amounts authorized to be appropriated or otherwise available for District of Columbia, Federal Payment for Defender Services in DC Courts for fiscal year 2024 may not exceed $46,005,000.
