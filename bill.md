# S. 4602

> To authorize the appropriation of amounts for the construction of a National Guard and Reserve Center at Fort Eisenhower, Georgia.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fort Eisenhower National Guard Support Act”.

## § 2. Authorization of amounts for construction of a National Guard and Reserve Center at Fort Eisenhower, Georgia

There is authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary of Defense for fiscal year 2025 $21,000,000 for the construction of a National Guard and Reserve Center at Fort Eisenhower, Georgia.
