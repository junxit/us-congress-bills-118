# H.R. 8589

> To require the United States Postal Service to implement recommendations from the Inspector General of the United States Postal Service for decreasing instances of delayed mail, improving staffing, and training, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Rural Mail Improvement Act”.

## § 2. Requirement for the Postal Service to implement certain recommendations of the Inspector General

Not later than one year after the date of the enactment of this Act, the United States Postal Service shall review and, to the extent practicable, implement the recommendations of the Inspector General of the United States Postal Service with respect to delayed mail, staffing, and training in the report by the Office of the Inspector General of the United States Postal Service entitled “Delivery in Northern Minnesota” (Report Number 24–048–R24) and dated May 6, 2024.
