# H.R. 4634

> To authorize appropriations for the operation of the Southwest Montana Veterans’ Home, located in Butte, Montana.

## § 1. Authorization of appropriations for operation of Southwest Montana Veterans’ Home, Butte, Montana; reduction of certain amounts

- **(a)** *Authorization of appropriations* There are authorized to be appropriated $5,000,000 for fiscal year 2024 for the operation of the Southwest Montana Veterans’ Home, Butte, Montana.
- **(b)** *Reduction of certain available amounts* The amounts available to the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to carry out the Electronic Health Modernization program of the Department of Veterans Affairs are hereby reduced by $5,000,000.
