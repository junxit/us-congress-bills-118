# H.R. 7290

> To prohibit corruption in the office of any State or local chief prosecutor.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Against Federal Funds for Allowing Inappropriate Relationships Act” or the “AFFAIR Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition

No Federal funds may be awarded to the office of any State or local chief prosecutor if any individual in such office has been convicted for engaging corruption or any other unlawful activity.
