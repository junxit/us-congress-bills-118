# H.R. 7990

> To extend the authorization for a large-scale water recycling and reuse grant program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Large-Scale Water Recycling Reauthorization and Investment Act of 2024”.

## § 2. Amendments to Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act

The Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act (Public Law 117–58) is amended—

- **(1)** in section 40901(4)(B), by striking “$450,000,000” and inserting “$1,000,000,000”; and
- **(2)** in section 40905—
  - **(A)** in subsection (c)(2), by striking “$500,000,000” and inserting “$1,000,000,000”; and
  - **(B)** in subsection (k), by striking “5 years after the date of enactment of this Act” and inserting “6 years after the date of enactment of the Large-Scale Water Recycling Reauthorization and Investment Act of 2024”.
