# S. 3370

> To reauthorize the program on prenatal and postnatal health of the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Promoting Maternal and Child Health Through Substance Use Prevention Act”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of program

Section 317L(d) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 247b–13(d)) is amended by striking “2019 through 2023” and inserting “2024 through 2028”.
