# H.R. 2116

> To provide for a limitation on availability of funds for Independent Agencies, General Services Administration, General Activities, Government-Wide Policy for fiscal year 2024.

## § 1. Limitation on availability of funds for Independent Agencies, General Services Administration, General Activities, Government-Wide Policy for fiscal year 2024

Notwithstanding any other provision of the law, amounts authorized to be appropriated or otherwise available for Independent Agencies, General Services Administration, General Activities, Government-Wide Policy for fiscal year 2024 may not exceed $60,000,000.
