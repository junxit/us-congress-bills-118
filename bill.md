# H.J. RES. 196

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Department of Defense relating to “Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement: Architect and Engineering Service Fees (DFARS Case 2024–D019)”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Department of Defense relating to “Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement: Architect and Engineering Service Fees (DFARS Case 2024–D019)” (89 Fed. Reg. 53502; published June 27, 2024) and such rule shall have no force or effect.
