# H.J. RES. 130

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Federal Railroad Administration relating to “Train Crew Size Safety Requirements”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Federal Railroad Administration relating to “Train Crew Size Safety Requirements” (89 Fed. Reg. 25052; published April 9, 2024), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
