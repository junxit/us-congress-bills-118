# H.J. RES. 185

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Securities and Exchange Commission relating to “Regulation S–P: Privacy of Consumer Financial Information and Safeguarding Customer Information”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Securities and Exchange Commission relating to “Regulation S–P: Privacy of Consumer Financial Information and Safeguarding Consumer Information” (89 Fed. Reg. 47688; published June 3, 2024), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
