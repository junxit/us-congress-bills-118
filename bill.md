# S. 2885

> To amend the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 to permit States to record in writing that applicants for supplemental nutrition assistance program benefits have signed an application through a recorded verbal assent over the telephone.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Streamlining Nutrition Paperwork Act of 2023” or the “SNP Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Amendments

Section 11(e)(2)(C) of the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 (7 U.S.C. 2020(e)(2)(C)) is amended—

- **(1)** in clause (ii)—
  - **(A)** in the heading, by inserting “or video phone” after “telephonic”; and
  - **(B)** by inserting “or by gesture over a video phone for hearing impaired applicants” before the period at the end; and
- **(2)** in clause (iii)(I), by inserting “as audio or in writing” after “record”.

## § 3. Effective date

This Act, and the amendment made by this Act, shall take effect on January 1, 2024.
