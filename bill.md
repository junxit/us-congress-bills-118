# H.R. 9444

> To amend the Reclamation Projects Authorization and Adjustment Act of 1992 to authorize appropriations for the Salton Sea Research Project.

## § 1. Authorization of appropriations

Section 1101(e) of the Reclamation Projects Authorization and Adjustment Act of 1992 (Public Law 102–575; 106 Stat. 4661(e)) is amended by striking “$13,000,000” and inserting “$300,000,000”.
