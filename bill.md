# H. RES. 967

> Amending the Rules of the House of Representatives to permit Members to vote by proxy in certain cases, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Permitting proxy voting for Members in certain cases

Clause 2 of rule III of the Rules of the House of Representatives is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (a), by striking “A Member may not” and inserting “Except as provided in paragraph (c), a Member may not”;
- **(2)** in paragraph (b), by striking “No other person may” and inserting “Except as provided in paragraph (c), no other person may”; and
