# H.R. 10056

> To amend the Older Americans Act of 1965 to authorize appropriations for fiscal years 2025 through 2028.

## § 1. Amendments to the Older Americans Act of 1965

The Older Americans Act of 1965 (42 U.S.C. 3001 et seq.) is amended—

- **(1)** in section 216 by striking “fiscal year 2024” each place it appears and inserting “each of the fiscal years 2024 through 2028”,
- **(2)** in section 303 by striking “fiscal year 2024” each place it appears and inserting “each of the fiscal years 2024 through 2028”,
- **(3)** in section 311(e) by striking “fiscal year 2024” and inserting “each of the fiscal years 2024 through 2028”,
- **(4)** in section 411(b) by striking “fiscal year 2024” each place it appears and inserting “each of the fiscal years 2024 through 2028”,
- **(5)** in section 517(a) by striking “fiscal year 2024” and inserting “each of the fiscal years 2024 through 2028”,
- **(6)** in section 643 by striking “fiscal year 2024” each place it appears and inserting “each of the fiscal years 2024 through 2028”, and
- **(7)** in section 702 by striking “fiscal year 2024” each place it appears and inserting “each of the fiscal years 2024 through 2028”.
