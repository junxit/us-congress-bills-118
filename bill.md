# S. RES. 780

> Recognizing August 1, 2024, as “National Poll Worker Recruitment Day”.

That the Senate—

- **(1)** recognizes August 1, 2024, as “National Poll Worker Recruitment Day”;
- **(2)** recognizes the need for, and appreciation of, the service of poll workers; and
- **(3)** encourages eligible people to help American citizens to vote in the 2024 elections by serving as poll workers.
