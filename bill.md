# S. 3089

> To amend the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2023, to expand the replacement of stolen EBT benefits under the supplemental nutrition assistance program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fairness for Victims of SNAP Skimming Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Replacement of stolen EBT benefits

Section 501(b)(2) of division HH of the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2023 (Public Law 117–328) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking subparagraph (A) and inserting the following:
  > - **(A)** shall be equal to the amount of benefits stolen from the household; and

- **(2)** in subparagraph (B), by striking “and” at the end; and
- **(3)** by striking subparagraph (C).
