# H.R. 6773

> To designate the Senator Dianne Feinstein Memorial Trail in Headwaters Forest Reserve, California.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Senator Dianne Feinstein Memorial Trail Act”.

## § 2. Senator Dianne Feinstein Memorial Trail

- **(a)** *In general* The Elk River Trail in Headwaters Forest Reserve, California, shall hereafter be known and designated as the “Senator Dianne Feinstein Memorial Trail”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Elk River Trail in Headwaters Forest Reserve shall be deemed to be a reference to the Senator Dianne Feinstein Memorial Trail.
