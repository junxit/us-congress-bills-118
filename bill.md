# S. 2537

> To amend the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 to improve access to the food distribution program on Indian reservations, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Tribal Nutrition Program Administration Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Elimination of the administrative and distribution cost sharing requirement

Section 4(b) of the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 (7 U.S.C. 2013(b)) is amended by striking paragraph (4) and inserting the following:

> - **(4)** *Administrative and distribution costs* The Secretary shall pay 100 percent of administrative costs and distribution costs on Indian reservations.
