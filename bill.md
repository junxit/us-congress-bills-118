# H.R. 1664

> To require the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System and the Federal Open Market Committee to consider inflation, employment, and financial stability when setting interest rates.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Financial Stability Mandate Act”.

## § 2. Interest rate considerations

Section 2A of the Federal Reserve Act is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “The Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System and the Federal Open Market Committee shall” and inserting the following:
  > - **(a)** *In general* The Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System and the Federal Open Market Committee shall

- **(2)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(b)** *Considerations* The Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System and the Federal Open Market Committee shall, when setting interest rates, consider inflation, employment, and financial stability.
