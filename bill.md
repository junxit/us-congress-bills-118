# H.R. 2182

> To provide for a limitation on availability of funds for U.S. Department of Interior, Department-Wide Programs, Wildland Fire Management for fiscal year 2024.

## § 1. Limitation on availability of funds for U.S. Department of Interior, Department-Wide Programs, Wildland Fire Management for fiscal year 2024

Notwithstanding any other provision of the law, amounts authorized to be appropriated or otherwise available for U.S. Department of Interior, Department-Wide Programs, Wildland Fire Management for fiscal year 2024 may not exceed $941,211,000.
