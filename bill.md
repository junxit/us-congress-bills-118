# H.R. 7602

> To amend the Defense Production Act of 1950 to prohibit the President from making provision for the purchase of solar panels under the authority of such Act, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Planes Not Panels Act”.

## § 2. Limitation on presidential action

Section 303(a) of the Defense Production Act of 1950 (50 U.S.C. 4533(a)) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(8)** *Solar panels*
>   - **(A)** *In general* The President may not use any authority under this section to make provision for purchases of or commitments to purchase solar panels.
>   - **(B)** *Solar panel defined* In this paragraph, the term “solar panel” means a crystalline silicon photovoltaic cell or module.
