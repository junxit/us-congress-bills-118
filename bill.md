# H.J. RES. 214

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the United States Fish and Wildlife Service relating to “Migratory Bird Hunting; Final 2024–25 Frameworks for Migratory Bird Hunting Regulations”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the United States Fish and Wildlife Service relating to “Migratory Bird Hunting; Final 2024–25 Frameworks for Migratory Bird Hunting Regulations” (89 Fed. Reg. 68500; published August 26, 2024), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
