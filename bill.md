# H. RES. 1360

> Honoring former fire department chief Corey Comperatore who was killed while shielding his family during the attempted assassination of the former President of the United States of America, Donald J. Trump.

## Preamble

- Whereas, Corey Comperatore was killed by the shooter at the rally for former President Donald Trump in Butler Pennsylvania on Saturday July 13th, 2024;
- Whereas, Corey Comperatore was a former chief firefighter of the Buffalo Township Volunteer Fire Company from Sarver, Pennsylvania;
- Whereas, Corey Comperatore was married with two daughters;
- Whereas, Corey Comperatore was killed while shielding his family from the gunfire that was aimed at assassinating former President Donald Trump; and
- Whereas, Corey Comperatore died a hero to his family and hero to his country for his service.

That the House of Representatives—

- **(1)** recognizes the life of Corey Comperatore and his service to the country;
- **(2)** recognizes that Corey Comperatore gave his life for his wife and two daughters;
- **(3)** calls on United States Government officials and politicians to—
  - **(A)** condemn the attempted assassination of former President Donald J. Trump; and
- **(4)** condemns political violence in all its forms.
