# H.R. 3338

> To authorize additional district judgeships for the district of Colorado, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Additional district judgeships for the district of Colorado

- **(a)** *Additional district judgeships* The President shall appoint, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, 1 additional district judge for the district of Colorado.

## § 2. Additional place for holding court for the district of Colorado

Section 85 of title 28, United States Code, is amended by inserting “Fort Collins,” after “Pueblo,”.
