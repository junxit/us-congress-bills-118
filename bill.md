# H.R. 10033

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the clean fuel production credit.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Expanding Clean Fuel Production Act of 2024”.

## § 2. Extension of clean fuel production credit

- **(a)** *In general* Section 45Z(g) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “December 31, 2027” and inserting “December 31, 2037”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to fuel sold after December 31, 2027.
