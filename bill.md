# H.R. 9034

> To prohibit the use of Federal funds for the salary of the Director of the United States Secret Service.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Secret Service Accountability Act”.

## § 2. Limitation on Federal funds for salary of Secret Service director

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, no Federal funds may be obligated or expended for the salary or expenses of Kimberly Cheatle, the Director of the United States Secret Service.
