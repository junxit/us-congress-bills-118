# H.R. 3840

> To amend the Public Health Service Act to authorize certain contracts and cooperative agreements with clinical laboratories for purposes of delivery of medical products to the Strategic National Stockpile.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Ensuring Sufficient Supply of Testing Act”.

## § 2. Contracts and cooperative agreements with clinical laboratories for the Strategic National Stockpile

Section 319F–2(a)(5)(A) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 247d–6b(a)(5)(A)) is amended by inserting after “contracts or cooperative agreements with vendors, which may include manufacturers or distributors of medical products,” the following: “as well as clinical laboratories,”.
