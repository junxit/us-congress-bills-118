# H.R. 7512

> To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to ensure implementation of real-time benefit tools under part D of the Medicare program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Real-Time Benefit Tool Implementation Act”.

## § 2. Ensuring implementation of real-time benefit tools under part D of the Medicare program

Section 1860D–4(o) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395w–104) is amended by striking paragraph (1) and inserting the following:

> - **(1)** *In general* Not later than January 1, 2027, a PDP sponsor of a prescription drug plan shall implement one or more electronic real-time benefit tools that meet the requirements described in paragraph (2).
