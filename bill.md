# H.R. 7242

> To amend the Small Business Act to increase the maximum gross loan amount for a loan made under section 7(a) of such Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Loans in our Neighborhoods Act”.

## § 2. Maximum gross loan amount for a 7(a) loan

Section 7(a)(3)(A) of the Small Business Act (15 U.S.C. 636(a)(3)(A)) is amended by striking “$5,000,000” and inserting “$10,000,000”.
