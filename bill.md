# S. 2755

> To extend the requirement to staff Department of Defense Education Activity schools to maintain maximum student-to-teacher ratios.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Success for Military Connected Students Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Staffing of Department of Defense Education Activity schools to maintain maximum student-to-teacher ratios

Section 589B(c) of the William M. (Mac) Thornberry National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2021 (Public Law 116–283; 134 Stat. 3659) is amended by striking “2023–2024 academic year” and inserting “2029–2030 academic year”.
