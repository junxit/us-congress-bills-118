# S. 1854

> To amend the Foreign Aid Transparency and Accountability Act of 2016 to require the information on the ForeignAssistance.gov website to be searchable.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Foreign Aid Transparency and Accountability Enhancement Act”.

## § 2. Modification of matters to be made publicly available

Section 4(b)(1) of the Foreign Aid Transparency and Accountability Act of 2016 (22 U.S.C. 2394c(b)(1)) is amended to read as follows:

> - **(1)** *In general* The information described in subsection (a)—
>   - **(A)** shall be published on a detailed basis, such as award-by-award; and
>   - **(B)** shall include, and shall be searchable by—
>     - **(i)** country or region, as appropriate;
>     - **(ii)** funding agency;
>     - **(iii)** managing agency;
>     - **(iv)** sector;
>     - **(v)** total award amount;
>     - **(vi)** appropriations account;
>     - **(vii)** fiscal year or fiscal years; and
>     - **(viii)** implementing partners, including prime and subawards.
