# H.R. 6099

> To amend the Immigration and Nationality Act to provide for the expedited removal of aliens who are unlawfully present in the United States and are residents of Palestinian-administered territories within Judea and Samaria or Gaza.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Evicting Violent Islamic Criminals That Entered Deviously Act” or the “EVICTED Act”.

## § 2. Expedited removal for unlawfully present aliens who are residents of Palestinian-administered territories within Judea and Samaria or Gaza

Section 235(b)(1)(A)(iii) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1225(b)(1)(A)(iii)) is amended—

- **(1)** in subclause (I), by inserting “or subclause (III)” after “subclause (II)”; and
- **(2)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(III)** *Additional aliens described* An alien described in this clause is an alien who is unlawfully present in the United States and is a resident of a Palestinian-administered territory within Judea and Samaria or Gaza.
