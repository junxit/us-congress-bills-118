# H.R. 6540

> To realign L–29 at full Federal expense for the Miccosukee Tribe to expand the area for the Tigertail camp, which is a residential area.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Tigertail Historical Tree Island Repatriation Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Central and Southern Florida Project, modification of the L-29 levee

In consultation with the South Florida Water Management District, the Secretary of the Army is authorized to design and construct, at full Federal expense, modifications to the L–29 Levee component of the Central and Southern Florida Project, to realign a portion of the L–29 Section 3 Levee Segment to reconfigure the levee in the vicinity of the Miccosukee Tribe of Indians of Florida’s Tigertail Camp, as expanded pursuant to the Miccosukee Tribe of Indians of Florida’s lease from the South Florida Water Management District. Any necessary lands, easements, rights-of-way, relocations, and dredge disposal areas (LERRDs) for such a modification would be provided by the South Florida Water Management District.
