# S. 693

> To amend chapter 131 of title 5, United States Code, to prohibit certain executive branch officials from holding individual stocks, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Eliminating Executive Branch Insider Trading Act”.

## § 2. Sense of Congress

It is the sense of Congress that executive branch officials should not have a personal financial interest in the outcome of Government policy decisions.
