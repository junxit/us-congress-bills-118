# S. RES. 326

> Recognizing August 23, 2023, as “National Poll Worker Recruitment Day”.

That the Senate—

- **(1)** recognizes August 23, 2023 , as “National Poll Worker Recruitment Day”;
- **(2)** recognizes the need for, and appreciation of, the service of poll workers; and
- **(3)** encourages eligible people to help America vote in the 2023 elections by serving as poll workers.
