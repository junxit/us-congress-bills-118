# H.R. 3647

> To amend the FAA Reauthorization Act of 2018 to reauthorize aviation workforce development programs, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Aviation Investment and Recruitment Act of 2023” or the “AIR Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Aviation Workforce Development Programs

Section 625(b)(1) of the FAA Reauthorization Act of 2018 (49 U.S.C. 40101 note) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “$5,000,000” each place it appears and inserting “$20,000,000”; and
- **(2)** by striking “2019 through 2023” each place it appears and inserting “2024 through 2028”.
