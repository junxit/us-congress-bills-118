# H.R. 5770

> To reauthorize certain United States Geological Survey water data enhancement programs.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Water Data Improvement Act”.

## § 2. Water data enhancement by United States Geological Survey

Section 9507 of the Omnibus Public Land Management Act of 2009 (42 U.S.C. 10367) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a)(6)—
  - **(A)** in subparagraph (A), by striking “2023” and inserting “2028”; and
  - **(B)** in subparagraph (B), by striking “2019” and inserting “2028”;
- **(2)** in subsection (b)(7), by striking “2023” and inserting “2028”; and
- **(3)** in subsection (d)(4), by striking “2019” and inserting “2028”.
