# H.R. 5272

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to modify the requirements relating to truthful cost or pricing data submissions, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Modification to truthful cost or pricing data submissions

Section 3705(b)(2)(B) of title 10, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in the second sentence, by inserting “and shall identify such offerors that incur a delay greater than 200 days in submitting such cost or pricing data” after “should-cost analysis”; and
- **(2)** by amending the third sentence to read as follows: “The Secretary of Defense shall include a public notation on such offerors in the system used by the Federal Government to monitor or record contractor integrity and performance.”.
