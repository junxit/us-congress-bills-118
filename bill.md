# S.J. RES. 70

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection relating to “Credit Card Penalty Fees (Regulation Z)”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection relating to “Credit Card Penalty Fees (Regulation Z)” (89 Fed. Reg. 19128 (March 15, 2024)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
