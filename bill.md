# H.R. 2779

> To repeal the corporate average fuel economy standards.

## § 1. Repealing the corporate average fuel economy standards

Chapter 329 of title 49, United States Code, and the item relating to such chapter in the analysis of chapters for subtitle VI of such title, are repealed.
