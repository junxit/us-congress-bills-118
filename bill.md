# H.R. 5795

> To direct the heads of Federal agencies and departments to publish certain data with respect to disaster relief, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Natural Disaster Transparency Enhancement Act”.

## § 2. Publication of disaster relief data

The head of each Federal agency or department that approves Federal funds for natural disaster relief shall publish and regularly update on the website of such agency or department a breakdown of—

- **(1)** the amount of funds approved with respect to each disaster; and
- **(2)** the amount of funds disbursed with respect to each disaster.
