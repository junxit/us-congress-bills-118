# H. RES. 706

> Amending the Rules of the House of Representatives to prohibit the appropriation of funds for the salary of any officer or employee of the Federal Government who is convicted of contempt of Congress.

## § 1. Short title

This resolution may be cited as the “Subpoena Compliance Accountability Act”.
