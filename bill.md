# S. 2178

> To extend the Chemical Facility Anti-Terrorism Standards Program of the Department of Homeland Security, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Protecting and Securing Chemical Facilities from Terrorist Attacks Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Extension of chemical facility anti-terrorism standards program of the department of homeland security

Section 5 of the Protecting and Securing Chemical Facilities from Terrorist Attacks Act of 2014 (6 U.S.C. 621 note) is amended by striking “July 27, 2023” and inserting “October 1, 2028”.
