# H.R. 9150

> To amend the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act to include wildfire smoke in the definition of major disaster.

## § 1. Short Title

This Act may be cited as “Keeping Families Safe from Wildfire Smoke Act”.

## § 2. Wildfire Smoke

Section 102(2) of the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act (42 U.S.C. 5122 (2)) is amended by inserting “wildfire smoke,” after “snowstorm,”.
