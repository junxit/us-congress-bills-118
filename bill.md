# H.J. RES. 79

> Relating to a national emergency declared by the President on May 11, 2004.

That pursuant to section 202 of the National Emergencies Act (50 U.S.C. 1622), the national emergency declared by the finding of the President on May 11, 2004, in Executive Order 13338, is hereby terminated.
