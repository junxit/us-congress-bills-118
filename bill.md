# H.R. 1213

> To amend the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2023 by repealing certain provisions relating to North Atlantic right whales.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Restoring Effective Science-based Conservation Under Environmental laws protecting Whales Act of 2023” or the “RESCUE Whales Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Repeal

Title I of division JJ of the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2023 (Public Law 117–328) is repealed.
