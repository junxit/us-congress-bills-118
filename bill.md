> To amend title 38, United States Code, to improve benefits administered by the Secretary of Veterans Affairs, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title; table of contents

## § 2. Definitions

In this Act:

- **(1)** *Claimant* The term claimant has the meaning given that term in section 5100 of title 38, United States Code.
- **(2)** *Department* The term Department means the Department of Veterans Affairs.
- **(3)** *Secretary* The term Secretary means the Secretary of Veterans Affairs.
