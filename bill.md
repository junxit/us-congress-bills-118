# H.R. 1817

> To prohibit Federal agencies from requiring lenders to estimate a borrower’s race or ethnicity via visual observation or surname.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Preventing Racial Profiling in Lending Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on estimating race or ethnicity

A Federal agency may not require any person or entity, when extending credit, to estimate a borrower’s race or ethnicity via visual observation or surname.
