# H.R. 5345

> To amend title 18, United States Code, to prevent the use of explosive materials to assault, resist, or impede certain officers or employees.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Curbing Offenses on Policing Services Act” or the “COPS Act” .

## § 2. Assaulting, resisting, or impeding certain officers or employees

Section 111(b) of title 18, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by inserting after “including” the following: “explosive materials, as defined in section 841, or”;
- **(2)** by striking “shall be fined under this title” and inserting “shall be fined up to $500,000, notwithstanding section 3571,”; and
- **(3)** by striking “20 years” and inserting “25 years”.
