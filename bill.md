# H.R. 6081

> To amend the short title of the Lake Tahoe Restoration Act to be the Dianne Feinstein Lake Tahoe Restoration Act.

## § 1. Dianne Feinstein Lake Tahoe Restoration Act

- **(a)** *Short title amendment* Section 1 of the Lake Tahoe Restoration Act (Public Law 106–506) is amended to read as follows:
  > ## § 1. Short title
  >
  > This Act may be cited as the “Dianne Feinstein Lake Tahoe Restoration Act”.

- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the “Lake Tahoe Restoration Act” (as a reference to Public Law 106–506) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Dianne Feinstein Lake Tahoe Restoration Act”.
