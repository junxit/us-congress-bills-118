# H.R. 9883

> To prohibit the implementation of the Proposed Resource Management Plan Amendment and Final Supplemental Environmental Impact Statement for the Miles City Field Office, Montana.

## § 1. Restriction on Proposed Resource Management Plan Amendment and Final Supplemental Environmental Impact Statement for the Miles City Field Office, Montana

The Secretary of the Interior may not finalize, implement, administer, or enforce the Proposed Resource Management Plan Amendment and Final Supplemental Environmental Impact Statement for the Miles City Field Office, Montana, referred to in the notice of availability published by the Bureau of Land Management titled “Proposed Resource Management Plan Amendment and Final Supplemental Environmental Impact Statement for the Miles City Field Office, Montana” (89 Fed. Reg. 43432; published May 17, 2024).
