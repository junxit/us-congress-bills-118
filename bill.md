# H.R. 5986

> To prohibit funds for the Palestinian-controlled West Bank or Gaza.

## § 1. Prohibition on funds for the Palestinian-controlled West Bank or Gaza

No Federal funds may be obligated or expended to provide funding or any other assistance for the Palestinian-controlled West Bank or Gaza.
