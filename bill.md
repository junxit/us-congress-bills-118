# H.R. 3885

> To amend the Energy Act of 2020 to include copper as a critical mineral, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Inclusion of copper as critical mineral

Section 7002(a)(3)(A) of the Energy Act of 2020 (30 U.S.C. 1606(a)(3)(A)) is amended to read as follows:

> - **(A)** *In general* The term “critical mineral” means—
>   - **(i)** copper; and
>   - **(ii)** any mineral, element, substance, or material designated as critical by the Secretary under subsection (c).
