# H.R. 8984

> To amend the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act to expand MBDA Business Centers near minority-serving institutions, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Supporting Diverse Entrepreneurs Act”.

## § 2. Expansion of MBDA Business Centers near minority-serving institutions

The Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act (Public Law 117–58) is amended—

- **(1)** in section 100114(d)(1)(B)—
  - **(A)** by redesignating clauses (ii) and (iii) as clauses (iii) and (iv), respectively; and
  - **(B)** by inserting after clause (i) the following:
    > - **(ii)** an area that is in or near a historically Black college or university or other minority-serving institution (as such term is defined in section 100301);

- **(2)** in section 100708, by striking “2025” and inserting “2024, and $125,000,000 for fiscal year 2025 and each fiscal year thereafter,”.
