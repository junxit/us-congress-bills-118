# H.R. 5123

> To amend the Fair Credit Reporting Act to modify the applicability of credit monitoring for active duty military consumers, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Credit Monitoring Integrity Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Amendment to applicability of credit monitoring for active duty military consumers

Section 605A(k) of the Fair Credit Reporting Act (15 U.S.C. 1681c–1(k)) is amended by striking paragraph (4).
