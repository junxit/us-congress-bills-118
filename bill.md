# H.R. 5308

> To reauthorize the Distance Learning and Telemedicine Program of the Department of Agriculture.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Rural Telehealth and Education Enhancement Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of the Distance Learning and Telemedicine Program

Section 2335A of the Food, Agriculture, Conservation, and Trade Act of 1990 (7 U.S.C. 950aaa–5) is amended by striking “2023” and inserting “2028”.
