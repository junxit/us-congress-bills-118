# H.R. 9476

> To condition the availability of Federal funds to Turkey on a formal apology for the attack on September 2, 2024, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Limitation on Federal funds

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, no Federal funds may be obligated, expended, or otherwise made available to Turkey until the Government of Turkey issues a formal apology for the attack on United States Marines on September 2, 2024.
