# H. RES. 1420

> Disapproving the rule submitted by the Secretary of Homeland Security relating to “Notice of implementation of the Keeping Families Together process.”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Secretary of Homeland Security relating to “Notice of implementation of the Keeping Families Together process.”, and such rule shall have no force or effect.
