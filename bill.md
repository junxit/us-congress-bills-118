# S. 601

> To amend the National Flood Insurance Act of 1968 to modify the amount by which the chargeable risk premium rate for flood insurance under that Act may be increased in any year.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Flood Insurance Affordability Act”.

## § 2. Annual limitation on premium increases

Section 1308(e)(1) of the National Flood Insurance Act of 1968 (42 U.S.C. 4015(e)(1)) is amended, in the matter preceding subparagraph (A), by striking “18 percent” and inserting “9 percent”.
