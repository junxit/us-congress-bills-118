# H. CON. RES. 79

> Authorizing the use of the rotunda of the Capitol for the National Prayer Breakfast.

## § 1. Use of rotunda for National Prayer Breakfast

- **(a)** *Authorization*
  - **(1)** *In general* The rotunda of the Capitol is authorized to be used on February 1, 2024, for the National Prayer Breakfast.
  - **(2)** *Food and beverage service* The authorization provided under paragraph (1) shall include the service of food and beverages at the event described in such paragraph.
- **(b)** *Preparations* Physical preparations for the conduct of the event described in subsection (a) shall be carried out in accordance with such conditions as may be prescribed by the Architect of the Capitol.
