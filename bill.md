# S. 467

> To modify the age requirement for the Student Incentive Payment Program of the State maritime academies.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Changing Age-Determined Eligibility To Student Incentive Payments Act” or the “CADETS Act”.

## § 2. Age requirement for the Student Incentive Payment Program of the State maritime academies

Section 51509 of title 46, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(i)** *Age requirement* The Secretary may make an agreement under this section only with a qualified student who will meet the age requirement for enlistment in the Navy Reserve at the time of graduation from the academy.
