# H. RES. 231

> Amending the Rules of the House of Representatives to require each Member, Delegate, Resident Commissioner, officer, and employee of the House of Representatives to complete a medical emergency preparedness training, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Mandatory medical emergency preparedness training

- **(a)** *In general* Rule XXIX of the Rules of the House of Representatives is amended by adding at the end the following:
- **(b)** *Effective date*
  - **(1)** *In general* This resolution and the amendments made by this resolution shall take effect on the date that is 90 days after the date of the adoption of this resolution.
  - **(2)** *Training deadline for current Congress* Not later than 90 days after the effective date specified in paragraph (1), an individual who is serving as a Member, Delegate, Resident Commissioner, officer, or employee of the House of Representatives as of such effective date shall complete the medical emergency preparedness training required under clause 5(a) of Rule XXIX of the Rules of the House of Representatives, as added by this resolution.
