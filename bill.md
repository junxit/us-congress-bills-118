# S. 4656

> To amend title 5, United States Code, concerning restrictions on the participation of certain Federal employees in partisan political activity, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Restrictions on partisan political activity

Section 7323(b)(2)(B)(i) of title 5, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in subclause (XIII), by striking “or” at the end;
- **(2)** in subclause (XIV), by striking “or” at the end; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(XV)** any Office of Inspector General established under chapter 4; or
  > - **(XVI)** any Office of Special Inspector General; or
