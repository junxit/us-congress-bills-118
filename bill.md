# S. 4382

> To amend the Water Resources Development Act of 1992 to provide for environmental infrastructure in East Point, Georgia.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “East Point Water Infrastructure Enhancement Act”.

## § 2. East Point, Georgia

Section 219(f)(136) of the Water Resources Development Act of 1992 (106 Stat. 4835; 113 Stat. 334; 121 Stat. 1261; 136 Stat. 3817) is amended by striking “$15,000,000” and inserting “$20,000,000”.
