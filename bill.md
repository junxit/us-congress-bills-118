# H.J. RES. 182

> Disapproving the rule submitted by the Department of Health and Human Services relating to “Designated Placement Requirements for LGBTQI plus Children”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Department of Health and Human Services relating to “Designated Placement Requirements for LGBTQI plus Children” published in the Federal Register on April 30, 2024 (89 Fed. Reg. 34818 et seq.), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
