# H. RES. 504

> Recognizing and honoring the 51 mobility Airmen from Joint Base Charleston who received the Distinguished Service Cross.

## Preamble

- Whereas 51 mobility Airmen from Joint Base Charleston served in Operation Allies Refuge, the evacuation of Afghanistan; and
- Whereas the Airmen’s heroic efforts during that Operation resulted in the award of the Distinguished Service Cross: Now, therefore, be it

That the House of Representatives recognizes and honors the 51 mobility Airmen from Joint Base Charleston who received the Distinguished Service Cross for heroic efforts during the Afghanistan evacuation Operation Allies Refuge.
