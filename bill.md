# S. 4384

> To amend the Water Resources Development Act of 1992 to provide for environmental infrastructure in Columbus, Henry, and Clayton Counties, Georgia.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Georgia Stormwater Management Act”.

## § 2. Columbus, Henry, and Clayton Counties, Georgia

Section 219(f) of the Water Resources Development Act of 1992 (106 Stat. 4835; 113 Stat. 334; 136 Stat. 3808) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(406)** *Columbus, Henry, and Clayton Counties, Georgia* $10,000,000 for environmental infrastructure, including water and wastewater infrastructure (including stormwater management), Columbus, Henry, and Clayton Counties, Georgia.
