# H.R. 6400

> To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to extend eligibility for certain payment increases for biosimilar biological products under the Medicare program.

## § 1. Extending eligibility for certain payment increases for biosimilar biological products under the Medicare program

Section 1847A(b)(8)(B)(ii)(II) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395w–3a(b)(8)(B)(ii)(II)) is amended by striking “2027” and inserting “2032”.
