# S. 2997

> To amend title 40, United States Code, to increase the mileage of the Appalachian Development Highway System to provide for improvements to and expansion of Corridor K in North Carolina, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Appalachian Development Highway System

- **(a)** *In general* Section 14501(a) of title 40, United States Code, is amended, in the second sentence, by striking “three thousand and ninety miles” and inserting “3,105 miles”.
- **(b)** *Purpose* The increase in mileage to the Appalachian Development Highway System made by the amendment made by subsection (a) shall be for improvements to and expansion of Corridor K in North Carolina.
