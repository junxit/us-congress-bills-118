# S. 3448

> To reauthorize the Director of the United States Holocaust Memorial Museum to support Holocaust education programs, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Never Again Education Reauthorization Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Reauthorization

Section 4(a) of the Never Again Education Act (Public Law 116–141; 134 Stat. 638) is amended by striking “each of the 4 succeeding fiscal years” and inserting “each succeeding fiscal year through fiscal year 2030”.
