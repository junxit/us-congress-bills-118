# S. 3027

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the energy credit for qualified fuel cell property.

## § 1. Extension of energy credit for qualified fuel cell property

Section 48(c)(1)(E) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “January 1, 2025” and inserting “January 1, 2033”.
