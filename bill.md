# S. 2898

> To amend the Food, Conservation, and Energy Act of 2008 to authorize the Secretary of Agriculture to authorize the use of certain grants to deliver peer-to-peer mental health support to individuals who are engaged in farming, ranching, farm work, and other occupations relating to agriculture.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Supporting Farmworkers' Mental Health Act”.

## § 2. Findings

Congress finds that farmworkers—

- **(1)** are vital to the economy and food supply of the United States; and
- **(2)** who are disproportionately affected by stress and anxiety, often lack access to behavioral health care services.

## § 3. Farm and Ranch Stress Assistance Network

Section 7522 of the Food, Conservation, and Energy Act of 2008 (7 U.S.C. 5936) is amended—

- **(1)** by inserting “farm work,” after “ranching,” each place it appears;
- **(2)** in subsection (b)—
  - **(A)** in paragraph (1)(D), by striking “or” at the end;
  - **(B)** by redesignating paragraph (2) as paragraph (3);
  - **(C)** by inserting after paragraph (1) the following:
    > - **(2)** to initiate, expand, or sustain programs that deliver peer-to-peer mental health support to individuals who are engaged in farming, ranching, farm work, and other occupations relating to agriculture; or

  - **(D)** in paragraph (3) (as so redesignated), by striking “paragraph (1) and subsection (a)” and inserting “paragraphs (1) and (2) and subsection (a)”; and
- **(3)** in subsection (d), by striking “2023” and inserting “2028”.
