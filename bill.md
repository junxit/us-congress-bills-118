# H.R. 7011

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 209 Main Street in Duncan, Arizona, as the “Sandra Day O’Connor Post Office”.

## § 1. Sandra Day O’Connor Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 209 Main Street in Duncan, Arizona, shall be known and designated as the “Sandra Day O’Connor Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Sandra Day O’Connor Post Office”.
