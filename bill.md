# H. RES. 628

> Eliminating the Office of Diversity and Inclusion of the House of Representatives, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Eliminating Office of Diversity and Inclusion

- **(a)** *In general* The Office of Diversity and Inclusion of the House of Representatives is hereby terminated.
- **(b)** *Conforming amendments to Rules of the House*
  - **(1)** *Conforming amendment relating to establishment of Office of Diversity and Inclusion* Rule II of the Rules of the House of Representatives is amended—
    - **(A)** by striking clause 9; and
    - **(B)** by redesignating clause 10 as clause 9.
  - **(2)** *Conforming amendment relating to oversight* Clause 4(d)(1)(A) of rule X of the Rules of the House of Representatives is amended—
    - **(A)** by striking “the Office of Diversity and Inclusion,”; and
    - **(B)** by striking “Office of Diversity and Inclusion,”.
