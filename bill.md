# S. 2182

> To amend the Agricultural Marketing Act of 1946 to establish a label designating fish harvested in the United States exclusive economic zone or navigable waters as wild USA seafood, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Wild USA Seafood Act of 2023.
