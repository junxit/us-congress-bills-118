# H.R. 1928

> To provide for a limitation on availability of funds for Department of Labor, Community Service Employment for Older Americans for fiscal year 2024.

## § 1. Limitation on availability of funds for Department of Labor, Community Service Employment for Older Americans for fiscal year 2024

Notwithstanding any other provision of the law, amounts authorized to be appropriated or otherwise available for Department of Labor, Community Service Employment for Older Americans for fiscal year 2024 may not exceed $400,000,000.
