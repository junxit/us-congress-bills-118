# H.R. 8909

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 82–6110 Mamalahoa Highway in Captain Cook, Hawaii, as the “Army 1st Lt. John Kuulei Kauhaihao Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Army 1st Lt. John Kuulei Kauhaihao Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 82–6110 Mamalahoa Highway in Captain Cook, Hawaii, shall be known and designated as the “Army 1st Lt. John Kuulei Kauhaihao Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Army 1st Lt. John Kuulei Kauhaihao Post Office Building”.
