# H.R. 2175

> To provide for a limitation on availability of funds for U.S. Department of Interior, Office of Surface Mining Reclamation and Enforcement, Regulation and Technology for fiscal year 2024.

## § 1. Limitation on availability of funds for U.S. Department of Interior, Office of Surface Mining Reclamation and Enforcement, Regulation and Technology for fiscal year 2024

Notwithstanding any other provision of the law, amounts authorized to be appropriated or otherwise available for U.S. Department of Interior, Office of Surface Mining Reclamation and Enforcement, Regulation and Technology for fiscal year 2024 may not exceed $115,804,000.
