# H. CON. RES. 116

> Authorizing the use of Emancipation Hall in the Capitol Visitor Center for a historic ceremony to commemorate Slavery Remembrance Day.

## § 1. Short title

This resolution may be cited as the “Original Resolution to Commemorate Slavery Remembrance Day in Emancipation Hall”.

## § 2. Use of Emancipation Hall for ceremony to commemorate Slavery Remembrance Day

- **(a)** *Authorization* Emancipation Hall in the Capitol Visitor Center is authorized to be used on August 10, 2024, for a ceremony to commemorate Slavery Remembrance Day.
- **(b)** *Preparations* Physical preparations for the conduct of the ceremony described in subsection (a) shall be carried out in accordance with such conditions as the Architect of the Capitol may prescribe.
