# S. RES. 336

> Prohibiting the imposition of vaccination requirements relating to COVID–19 for Senate Pages.

## § 1. Prohibition on COVID–19 vaccination requirements for Senate Pages

A Senate Page or applicant to be a Senate Page may not be required to receive a vaccination for COVID–19.
