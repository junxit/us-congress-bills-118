# S. 4465

> To reauthorize the Uyghur Human Rights Policy Act of 2020.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Uyghur Human Rights Policy Reauthorization Act of 2024”.

## § 2. Reauthorization

Section 6(h) of the Uyghur Human Rights Policy Act of 2020 (Public Law 116–145; 22 U.S.C. 6901 note) is amended by striking “5 years after” and inserting “10 years after”.
