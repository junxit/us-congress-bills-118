# H.R. 8377

> To amend the Advancing Research to Prevent Suicide Act to expand the areas of focus regarding childhood suicide, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Youth Suicide Prevention Research Act”.

## § 2. NSF research regarding childhood suicide

Subsection (a) of section 3 of the Advancing Research to Prevent Suicide Act is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (4), by striking “and” after the semicolon;
- **(2)** by redesignating paragraph (5) as paragraph (7); and
- **(3)** by inserting after paragraph (4) the following new paragraphs:
  > - **(5)** basic understanding of the role of adverse childhood experiences;
  > - **(6)** basic understanding of the role of toxic stress in childhood; and
