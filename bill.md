# S. 3870

> To amend the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act to expand eligibility for certain financial assistance for the acquisition of slip-on tanker units for wildland firefighting.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Slip-on Tanks for Tribes Act of 2024”.

## § 2. Financial assistance for acquisition of firefighting slip-on tanker units

Section 40803(c)(5) of the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act (16 U.S.C. 6592(c)(5)) is amended by inserting “and Indian Tribes” after “local governments”.
