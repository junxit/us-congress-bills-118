# H.R. 8803

> To amend the Immigration and Nationality Act to increase the civil money penalty for hiring, recruiting, and referral violations.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Illegal Labor Accountability Act of 2024”.

## § 2. Increase of civil money penalty for hiring, recruiting, and referral violations

Section 274A(e)(4)(A) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1324a(e)(4)(A)) is amended—

- **(1)** in clause (i)—
  - **(A)** by striking “$250” and inserting “$750”; and
  - **(B)** by striking “$2,000” and inserting “$6,000”;
- **(2)** in clause (ii)—
  - **(A)** by striking “$2,000” and inserting “$6,000”; and
  - **(B)** by striking “$5,000” and inserting “$15,000”; and
- **(3)** clause (iii)—
  - **(A)** by striking “$3,000” and inserting “$9,000”; and
  - **(B)** by striking “$10,000” and inserting “$30,000”.
