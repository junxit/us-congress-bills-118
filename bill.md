# S. 5322

> To amend the United States Sentencing Guidelines applicable to human smuggling offenses, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Border Smuggling Crackdown Act”.

## § 2. Amendment of sentencing guidelines

Section 2L1.1 of the United States Sentencing Guidelines Manual is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (b)—
  - **(A)** by striking paragraph (2) and inserting the following:
    > - **(2)** If the offense involved the smuggling, transporting, or harboring of two or more unlawful aliens, increase as follows:

  - **(B)** by striking paragraph (7) and inserting the following:
    > - **(7)** If any person died or sustained bodily injury, increase the offense level according to the seriousness of the injury:

- **(2)** in Application Note 7 of the Commentary by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(D)** The offense involves injury to, or the death of, a substantial number of individuals.
