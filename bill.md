# H.R. 4043

> To amend the Save Our Seas 2.0 Act to make the District of Columbia eligible for certain wastewater infrastructure grants, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Wastewater infrastructure grants

Section 302(c)(1) of the Save Our Seas 2.0 Act (33 U.S.C. 4282) is amended by inserting “, the District of Columbia,” before “or Indian Tribes”.
