# S. 897

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to make a permanent increase in the number of judges presiding over the United States Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Expedite Veteran Appeals Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Permanent increase in number of judges presiding over Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims

Section 7253(a) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “seven” and inserting “nine”.
