# H.R. 2531

> To amend the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2016 to extend the authority relating to cooperation between the United States and Israel on anti-tunnel defense capabilities.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “United States-Israel Anti-Tunnel Cooperation Extension Act”.

## § 2. Extension of United States-Israel anti-tunnel cooperation

Section 1279(f) of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2016 (22 U.S.C. 8606 note) is amended by striking “December 31, 2024” and inserting “December 31, 2026”.
