# H.R. 3641

> To direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to rescind guidelines and ensure the removal of signs and guidance relating to the COVID–19 pandemic.

## § 1. Termination of Department of Veterans Affairs COVID–19 guidelines

The Secretary of Veterans Affairs shall immediately—

- **(1)** rescind any and all guidelines relating to the COVID–19 pandemic; and
- **(2)** ensure the removal of all signs and posted guidance related to COVID–19 at Department of Veterans Affairs facilities, including those regarding masking and social distancing.
