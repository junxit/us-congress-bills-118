# H.J. RES. 219

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Department of Health and Human Services relating to “Supporting the Head Start Workforce and Consistent Quality Programming”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Department of Health and Human Services relating to “Supporting the Head Start Workforce and Consistent Quality Programming” (89 Fed. Reg. 67720; published August 21, 2024), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
