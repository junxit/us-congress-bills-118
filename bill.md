# H.R. 9798

> To provide a short title for the law commonly referred to as the “Inflation Reduction Act”.

## § 1. Short title for Inflation Reduction Act

Public Law 117–169 (commonly referred to as the “Inflation Reduction Act)” is amended by inserting before title I the following:

> ## § 1. Short title
>
> This Act may be referred to as the “Lied about Inflation and Energy Dependence Act of 2022” or the “LIED Act of 2022”.
