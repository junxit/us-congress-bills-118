# H.R. 8778

> To waive the fee for the issuance of a passport for a family member of a member of the Armed Forces who is in a hospital or medical facility abroad, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “No Passport Fees for Heroes’ Families Act”.

## § 2. Waiver of passport fees for certain individuals

Paragraph (2) of section 1(a) of the Passport Act of June 4, 1920 (22 U.S.C. 214) is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating subparagraphs (D) and (E) as subparagraphs (E) and (F), respectively;
- **(2)** by inserting after subparagraph (C) the following new subparagraph:
  > - **(D)** from a husband, wife, domestic partner, child, parent, grandparent, brother, or sister of a member of the Armed Forces proceeding abroad to visit such member in a hospital or other medical facility;

- **(3)** in subparagraph (E), as so redesignated, by inserting “or” after the semicolon.
