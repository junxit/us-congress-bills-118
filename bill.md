# H.J. RES. 117

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to “Reconsideration of the National Ambient Air Quality Standards for Particulate Matter”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to “Reconsideration of the National Ambient Air Quality Standards for Particulate Matter” (89 Fed. Reg. 16202 (March 6, 2024)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
