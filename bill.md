# H. RES. 1533

> Reaffirming United States support of the State of Israel one year after the October 7, 2023, attacks.

That the House of Representatives—

- **(1)** reaffirms the United States strong and continued support of Israel one year following the October 7, 2023, surprise attacks on Israel;
- **(2)** condemns Hamas’ brutal war against Israel; and
- **(3)** calls on Hamas to immediately cease these violent attacks and safely release all living hostages and return the bodies of deceased hostages.
