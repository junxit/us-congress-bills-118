# H.R. 5194

> To prohibit Federal funds from being used to pay the salary of John Luman Smith.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Yanking Outlays for an Unethical, Ruthless Enterprise that Fraudulently Impedes Robust Electoral Debate Act” or the “YOU’RE FIRED Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on funding to provide a salary to John Luman Smith

No Federal funds may be used to provide a salary for John Luman Smith, Special Counsel for the Department of Justice.
