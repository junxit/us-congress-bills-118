# H.R. 9432

> To amend the United States Semiquincentennial Commission Act of 2016 to modify the requirements for the time capsule required by such Act, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Time capsule requirements under United States Semiquincentennial Commission Act of 2016

Section 7(f)(1) of the United States Semiquincentennial Commission Act of 2016 (Public Law 114–196) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking subparagraph (A) and inserting the following:
  > - **(A)** to be installed in Independence National Historical Park on July 4, 2026; and

- **(2)** in subparagraph (B), by striking “unearthed” and inserting “opened”.
