# H.R. 10400

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to clarify the definition of applicable educational institution for purposes of the excise tax based on the income of private colleges and universities.

## § 1. Clarification of definition of applicable educational institution for purposes of excise tax based on income of private colleges and universities

- **(a)** *In general* Section 4968(b)(1)(A) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by inserting “below the graduate level” after “500 tuition-paying students”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to taxable years beginning after December 31, 2024.
