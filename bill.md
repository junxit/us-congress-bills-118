# H.R. 9014

> To amend title 5, United States Code, to establish and clarify the applicable statute of limitations for persons seeking remedy for legal wrongs because of agency action or adversely affected by agency action.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Corner Post Reversal Act”.

## § 2. Right of review

Section 702 of title 5, United States Code, is amended by inserting “Except as otherwise expressly provided by law, an action under this section shall be commenced within 6 years after the date on which the relevant agency action was finalized.” after “judicial review thereof.”.
