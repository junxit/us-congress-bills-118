# H.R. 489

> To provide that the notice titled “Designating Aliens for Expedited Removal” shall be given the full force and effect of law, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Expedited Removal Codification Act of 2023”.

## § 2. Designating aliens for expedited removal

The notice titled, “Designating Aliens for Expedited Removal”, issued by the Department of Homeland Security on July 23, 2019 (84 Fed. Reg. 35409), shall have the full force and effect of law.
