# H.R. 4991

> To direct the Secretary of Homeland Security to commence a rule making to establish a U.S. Customs and Border Protection international port of entry at the Port of Stockton and for other purposes.

## § 1. Rule making to establish a U.S. Customs and Border Protection international port of entry at the Port of Stockton

The Secretary of Homeland Security shall commence a rule making to establish a U.S. Customs and Border Protection international port of entry at the Port of Stockton in the State of California.
